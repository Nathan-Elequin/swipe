The unanswered write-up from this fight is below.

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Move Pocketwatch to Special Item, Bucket 1. “Select any Monster. That Monster Attacks your Enemy.” The player picks a monster on the wheel, including the current one. That monster performs its attack against the current enemy the same way it would if the player had pressed end combat facing that other monster. Effects that would hit the player in that imagined combat hit the current enemy instead. It always uses that monster’s Attack from before Pocketwatch was used. The hypnotized monster is not defeated, because this is not a real combat. Hornet Swarm attacks twice. Dire Wolf attacks equal to the player’s HP. Damsel triggers both neighbors to attack the current enemy, in the same order as normal. Goblin still cannot take attack damage. Bodyguard and King still move attack damage. If the current enemy is Needleback, or has a similar on-attacked ability, that hits the hypnotized monster. After the pick, a second monster callout appears, mirrored so the hypnotized monster faces the current enemy. The hypnotized sprite looks hypnotized with the pocketwatch in front of its face, then attacks, and the current enemy takes that attack the same way they take attack damage from other items.

**Restate (what the player does):**

During a fight, you click Pocketwatch, then click any living monster on the wheel, including the one you are facing. Pocketwatch is used up. The monster you clicked (hypnotized) swings at the monster you are facing, using the Attack number that was on the hypnotized plate before you clicked Pocketwatch. Extra swings and redirect rules still run. The hypnotized monster stays on the wheel. If this drops the current enemy to 0 HP, the fight ends the same way a Shield-then-Knife kill already ends. You do not click Done to finish that kill.

**Where it happens in a round:** Fight only. Used up. Click the card, then click a monster. Attack number is a snapshot from before this use.
