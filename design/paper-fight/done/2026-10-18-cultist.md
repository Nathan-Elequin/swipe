# Paper Fight — Cultist / Blood Pact

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Monster, Tier 1, Bucket 8, Cultist (image: cultist in a dark robe). 2 HP, 2 Atk. Call name Blood Pact. Ability: When you Spin me or my Neighbor, Gain 1 Blood Pact. Blood Pact is a token item, black card like Egg. Ability: You and your Enemy lose 2 HP.

**Restate (what the player does):** They Spin. If the wheel lands on Cultist, or on whoever is sitting next to Cultist, a Blood Pact card appears in the hotbar (black token, like Spider Egg). Cultist still fights at 2 Attack / 2 HP. Later in that fight they can click Blood Pact. They lose 2 HP and the facing enemy loses 2 HP. The card is used up. If that drops the enemy to 0, the fight ends. The token stays in hand between fights until they click it. It is not loot; Cultist creates it.

**Where it happens in a round:** Grant happens on Spin, after the wheel lands, by itself — same family as Spider Queen laying an Egg, not an overlay like Merchant. They do not pick a target for the grant. Blood Pact is clicked during a fight, like Egg or Healing Potion’s timing (click, no second target). Used up on click. The 2 is a fixed number, not a copy that keeps updating.

---

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- **Spider Egg / Spider Queen** — Egg is the look-alike: token, not drafted, black card, created on Spin. Queen does not lay on the Spin that lands on her; Cultist *does* grant when you land on them. Queen skips a full hand; same fork here. Egg is “Take 2 Attack, Block works.” Blood Pact says lose HP, which is the other family (Napalm / poison tick).
- **Marble** — also a token created by another card. Blood Pact is not Marble (no random Attack/Block, no grow-on-Spin).
- **Quiet Bell** — silences the facing monster. Queen still lays from other living Queens. If you Bell Cultist, does that Cultist still grant? If you Bell their neighbor, the Cultist is not silenced.
- **Grappling Hook / Damsel** — those are not a Spin of the neighbor. Cultist does not grant when Damsel’s neighbor Attack hits them, and Hook-swap is not a Spin. (Already locked on Worm.)
- **Flee / Smoke / Triclops Always Spin / Hex landing / Challenge click** — those are Spins. A landing on Cultist or a Cultist’s neighbor grants.
- **Merchant / Hired Hand / Cube / Almighty Worm overlays** — if that overlay monster is Cultist’s neighbor, the grant happens, then the overlay. You cannot click Blood Pact during the overlay. After Continue, you can.
- **Goblin** — cannot take Attack. If Blood Pact is Attack, Goblin takes 0 and you still lose 2. If it is HP loss, Goblin loses 2.
- **Anvil** — cannot lose more than 1 HP from one source, including “other HP loss.” A 2-chip would become 1 on Anvil either way.
- **Dragon pierce / Double-Buckler** — only matter if your 2 is Attack. HP chip skips both.
- **Block / Shield / Helmet** — Egg’s 2 is stopped by Block. Poison / Napalm / Ale Flagon tick is not. Blood Pact’s sentence matches the second family.
- **Spiked Flail / Bloody Wraps / Breastplate** — they watch you lose HP. An HP chip of 2 would tick them. Attack fully blocked would not.
- **Healing Potion** — if your 2 drops you to 0, Potion can still save. If the enemy also hit 0, you live and they are slain.
- **Hornet / Queen / Shambling Mound / Kobold** — extra swings are after you maybe clicked the pact. Pact is not an extra swing.
- **Wraith / Commander / Lich / Slime** — Cultist is 2/2 plus those auras. Slime’s ATK follows HP, so a 2-chip drops their ATK.
- **Prism** — Attack at Cultist hits neighbors instead. Blood Pact still names “your Enemy” (the one you spun), not Prism’s neighbors.
- **Mimic** — if Blood Pact drops them to 0 before they Attack, you still get the gift.
- **Cleric** — steal on Spin still happens in the existing order. A new token can be the lowest-tier steal.
- **Zombie** — no Gain. The grant is Gain. Quiet Bell / silenced Zombie is a separate card; if you Spin Zombie next to Cultist, Cultist still grants unless we skip all grants on a Zombie landing.
- **Hex** — Unplayable; Blood Pact is clickable. Both can sit together.
- **Basilisk petrify** — a petrified Blood Pact cannot be clicked until it thaws, same as other click cards.
- **Hand full (Challenge 10)** — Queen skips the Egg. Same fork for Blood Pact.
- **Almighty Worm** — overlay can eat Blood Pact as a discard, same as any hand card.
- **Empty alcove / last owner / tutorial / Developer Test** — empty seats are not neighbors who can be Spun. Tutorial kit does not seat Cultist. Developer Test Bucket 8 can (Triclops is also Bucket 8). Last owner: if they are Cultist or next to one, grant still happens.

**Generous reading:** Every living Cultist who is the landing, or sitting next to the landing, grants one Blood Pact (two Cultists beside each other can give two on one Spin). Quiet Bell on a neighbor still lets the unsileced Cultist grant. Blood Pact is a 2 HP chip on both of you: Block does not stop it, Goblin takes it, Flail ticks. Both chips happen, then Potion, then slay/death. You can suicide-kill a 2 HP enemy. Full hand still skips the grant.

**Stingy reading:** One Blood Pact per Spin even if two Cultists qualify. Quiet Bell skips every Cultist grant that Spin. “Lose 2 HP” is Attack 2 at you (Block works, like Egg) and Attack 2 at them (Goblin immune). If you would hit 0, you die first and the enemy never loses HP (no suicide-kill without Potion).

**Answers:**
1. One Blood Pact per qualifying Cultist.
2. HP chip of 2 each — Block does not stop it, Goblin takes it, Flail ticks.
3. The click still happens at 1 or 2 HP. Potion can save. If it does not, you die and they still lose 2.
4. Quiet Bell only skips a silenced Cultist (Queen-like). Bell their neighbor: they still grant.
