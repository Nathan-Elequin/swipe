# Paper Fight — Leech League spawn takeover

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Same stats, same Blood Rise ability, add this line at the end: “When I spawn, I take over another Monster.” When it enters the dungeon, it looks for the weakest monster (by tier), then creates an exact copy of itself in that monster’s position on the wheel. This takeover does not trigger any effects in the game; it is as if that monster was instantly removed and the 2nd Leech League was put in its place. Intended behavior is that there will always be 2 Leech Leagues as soon as the first enters. If the player Ejects a Leech League, they could theoretically end up with 3. If there are no other monsters to take over, then the ability just fails.

**Rulings (2026-11-01):**
- Fire once the dungeon is actually going (Adventure mill finished / Challenge Begin / Dev Test Play), before Spin. Slash portrait, then a radiating line, then the victim is removed and a new Leech League falls into the seat.
- Mid-fight: skip the face you are fighting. If that was the only other monster, fail.
- Skip other Leech Leagues as victims.
- Tied lowest catalog tier: random.
- Copy is always a fresh printed 2 HP / 4 Attack.
- Quiet Bell on the spawning Leech League blocks takeover.
- The planted copy does not spawn-takeover. Victim is silent (no Defeat / Flee / Hatch / gift / Devour / enter gains).
- Catapult return is a new spawn and can make a third.
