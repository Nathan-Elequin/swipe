# Paper Fight — Oligarch

Implemented 2026-10-28. Bucket 9, tier 2. No other mill card moved.

**Pitch:** 6 HP, 5 Attack. Wealth Tax. When she enters the dungeon, you gain 2 items. Whenever you Spin, she forces a random playable item. Entry line: Here, I bought you something nice.

**Nathan's answers:**

1. If the item needs a second click, open the normal choice and wait.
2. The two gifts are fresh copies from this mode's draftable items. No Smoke Bomb, no tokens, no curses.
3. Empty slots only. A full bar gets nothing.
4. She also pays when she appears later. Catapult redrawing her counts. Polymorph counts as entering for every card that already cares about entering.
5. The force happens after Merchant, the Cube, Hired Hand, and the Worm have been answered.
6. Smoke Bomb cannot be the forced item.
7. Harpy, Kobold, and the other item-use watchers see the force.
8. Each living copy pays. Two gifts per entry, one forced item per Spin, seat order.

**Settled while coding:**

- No empty slot, or no legal gift in the unlocked draft list: no slash and no line.
- One empty slot: one item, and the line.
- A silenced Oligarch does not give items and does not force.
- The force is only armed on a Spin that is still a fight when the On Spin chain finishes. A Spin that already ended does not spend an item.
- Echo Bell copying Smoke Bomb is skipped, same as Smoke Bomb.
- Plague Doctor and Fairy already work by being on the wheel. Polymorph now uses the same enter hook as a newly seated monster, so Death Bell's toll, Rust Bucket's Rust, and Oligarch's gift run for the new body. Beggar's intro still starts from the next refresh.
