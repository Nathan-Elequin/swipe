Rework into a Move, then 3 Attack. Move means shift to a Neighbor without Spinning. The card shows an arrow curving up and to the right. No Keep.

**What he answered:**

1. The step happens first. Then the 3 Attack hits the monster you are now facing (the one up and to the right, counter-clockwise).
2. That neighbor and the adventure Move-right button are the same direction. If they were not, fix the button.
3. If only one living monster is left, the Move does not happen and the 3 Attack still does.

**What the click does now:**

Used up. No Keep. Does not spend an adventure Move. Move-right walks counter-clockwise, which is up and to the right from the monster at the bottom of the wheel.
