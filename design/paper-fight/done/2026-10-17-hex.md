# Paper Fight — Hex

**Pitch:** Unplayable T2 B6. Skull + countdown from 6. Each Spin −1. Flashes red at 1. On the Spin that hits 0, Defeat the landed enemy (Boot-style, no HP chip). Any Spin counts (Flee, Smoke, auto-spin).

**Nathan’s picks:**
1. Two Hexes at 0 on the same Spin: both spend, one Defeat.
2. Gargoyle: Hex Defeats them. Nothing prevents Defeat; Commander can still Flee instead of leaving.
3. Poison: both fire, Hex first (Defeat makes the poison chip useless this landing).
4. New dungeon: countdown resets to 6.
