# Paper Fight — hide Undo after Wealth Tax, random item output, and Flee

Answered 2026-11-02. Shipped on `playtest/latest`.

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**
Oligarch Wealth Tax forced an item, then Undo after resolve gave full control again. Turn Undo off every time Wealth Tax triggers. Also turn Undo off until the next action for random chance the player does not control (Spin already does this; Marbles and Jar of Grease should too) and for items that Flee, because Flee is a Spin.

**Restate (what the player does):**
After those resolves, Undo is hidden until the next voluntary click. They cannot rewind the random or forced result. The next thing they choose can be undone as usual.

**Answers:**
1. Jar O' Grease true rule is Move 2 in a random direction and Keep. Hide Undo after that roll.
2. Hide Undo on random draws too (Ale Flagon, Catapult, Grappling Hook, Sling, Smoke, Chaos).
3. Item Flee and monster Flee (Commander, Hired Hand, Cowardly King, Petty King, Phoenix / Egg, Mouse).
4. Wealth Tax: hide Undo after any forced use, even a Shield, until the next thing they click.
5. Flying Saucer vs Ball and Chain: leave Undo on (no Spin, Block is deterministic).
6. Echo Bell copying Marble: hide Undo, same as Marble.
