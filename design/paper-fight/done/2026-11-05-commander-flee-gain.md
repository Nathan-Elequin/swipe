# Paper Fight — Petty King flee keeps his HP, Commander gains 3

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Petty King, call Vindictive. Ability: "Every time I lose HP, I command my Neighbor to the right to Attack. When I Attack, I Flee instead of being Defeated."

Any time anything Flees, for any reason, it keeps the stats it has. Petty King at 3 HP who Flees is still at 3 HP. He is not put back to 8, and he is not put at 0.

Commander, rewrite the Ability to: "My Neighbors Always have +2 Attack. The first time I would be Defeated, I Flee and Gain 3 HP."

If damage would drop Commander to 0, he Flees and ends at 3 HP (he heals back to his starting 3). If something else Defeats him without taking that HP (The Boot), he keeps the HP he had and Gains 3, so a full 3 HP Commander ends at 6.

**Restate (what the player does):**

You do not click either card. They sit on the wheel.

Petty King is 8 HP and 2 Attack. When his HP number goes down, the living monster one seat to his right Attacks you. When Petty King himself Attacks, he then leaves the fight by Fleeing, and the HP and Attack you already see stay on him. The wheel spins at random. He is not removed.

Commander is 3 HP and 3 Attack. While he is alive on the wheel, his neighbors have +2 Attack. The first time he would be Defeated, he is not removed. He Flees, and his HP changes by the rule above. The second time he would be Defeated, he is actually Defeated.

**Where it happens in a round:** During the fight. No extra click. The Flee number is copied once at that moment. It does not keep updating back toward printed max.

Petty King’s right-hand command is the same moment as today: after the current item finishes, one command per HP loss. The Flee is the moment he Attacks.

Commander’s Flee is the moment a Defeat would remove him. Damage that does not drop him to 0 does not Flee him.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- **Petty King** — Today, if you press Done and he still has HP, he Attacks and then Flees. A killing blow still Defeats him (the Flee check refuses 0 HP). Chip damage already stays on the wheel object. The new sentence adds the Flee to the Ability, and he must still show that chipped HP the next time you meet him.
- **Commander** — Today the first Defeat Flees him and puts HP back to the moment before the killing hit (a Commander at 2 who takes 4 leaves at 2). The new sentence replaces that. Damage that would Defeat him ends at 3. The Boot ends at current HP + 3. The second Defeat still kills him.
- **The Boot** and other Defeat items — They Defeat without changing HP. On Commander, that is the +3 case. On Petty King, he did not Attack, so he is Defeated. He does not Flee and he does not Gain HP. Defeat still does not command his right-hand neighbor, because he did not lose HP.
- **Ball and Chain** — Cancels a Flee. Today Commander stays in the fight at the pre-hit HP, and that first Flee is used up. The new Gain 3 makes this a different number.
- **Hydra (Hungry Heads)** — A living monster at 3 HP or less whose HP went down can be Defeated. Commander’s damage Flee ends at exactly 3.
- **Bodyguard** — Can still die taking the hit that would have Defeated Commander. This pitch does not bring Bodyguard back.
- **Damsel, Hired Hand, a second Petty King** — They can make Petty King Attack when you did not press Done. "When I Attack" might Flee him then, or only after Done.
- **Mouse, Triclops, Sandbag** — They already lose a printed 2 and then Flee. They keep the stats from after that printed cost. This fight does not change those costs.
- **Phoenix Egg** — Still hatches into a Phoenix at the Phoenix’s printed HP. The egg body is replaced.
- **Fungal Colony, Hired Hand** — They Flee on their own rules and keep the HP they have when they run.
- **Shuriken / Flying Saucer / Energy Drink** — You Flee. This rule is about the monster who Flees.
- **Goblin** — Immune to Attack, so an Attack does not drop his HP and does not command anyone through Petty King.
- **Lich** — A real Defeat still chips him. A Flee is not a Defeat.
- **Quiet Bell** — A silenced monster does not Flee and does not command.
- **Always** (the keyword) — Hover text is "cannot be changed in any other way." Commander’s new sentence capitalizes Always. Today the +2 is an aura: neighbors can still be changed by other cards, and the +2 drops if Commander is gone or silenced.
