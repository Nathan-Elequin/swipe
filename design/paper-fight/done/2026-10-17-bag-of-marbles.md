**Nathan’s picks:**
1. Fill always to 10, even in the 50-seat dungeon.
2. Bag is fight only.
3. Each Marble has its own number. A later Bag’s new ones start at 0.
4. A Marble at 0 can be clicked for 0 Attack or 0 Block and is used up.
5. Not an Attack item until it declares Attack. A Block declare never counted as Attack.
6. Facing Basilisk: Bag is spent, Gain is blocked, you get no Marbles.
7. Next dungeon: numbers reset to 0.
