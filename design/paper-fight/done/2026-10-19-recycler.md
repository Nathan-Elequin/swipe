# Paper Fight — Recycler

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Recycler. Every 5th Item Discarded is returned to your Hand. Static number counting down from 5 (always starts at 5 upon being drafted). Only counts down when an item is actually Discarded (does not count Keep or Use). DOES count other Discard effects like Bell, Cube, playing Keep cards and failing their keep condition. When it is at 1 (telegraphing the next item discarded), it gets a flashing aura on the card (perhaps replace 1 with a loop arrow) to loudly show that the next item discarded will return. When this happens, it is discarded (moving the countdown back to 5) but also causing the player to immediately re-acquire that item into their hotbar as if it was the first time they got it (eg scaling effects don't retain their stats like Grief Jar).

**Restate (what the player does):** Recycler sits in the hotbar showing 5. You do not click it. Each time some other rule Discards an item (Death Bell, Gelatinous Cube, Worm, Challenge throwing one away, a Keep card that fails Keep), the number drops 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1. Clicking an item to Use it does not drop the number, even if that card then leaves. Keep that succeeds does not drop it. At 1 the card flashes. The next Discard comes back into the hotbar as a brand-new copy (Grief Jar’s stored Block is gone, Sai starts at 2 again). Recycler’s number goes back to 5. It stays for later Discards unless something Discards Recycler itself.

**Where it happens in a round:** Passive, any time an item actually leaves as a Discard — fight, Cube feed, Bell tax, Worm hunger, Challenge full-hand throw, Keep-fail after Done. Not a click. The number lives on this copy until it resets or the card is gone. Fresh copy on return: stats are not remembered.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- **Death Bell** — Defeat: Discard 1 Item. That pick ticks Recycler. If Recycler is at 1, the discarded card comes back (Bell already ate; you still paid the tax, then get the card again).
- **Gelatinous Cube** — eats an item. Same as Bell.
- **Almighty Worm** — optional Discards that shrink it. Each sacrifice ticks. Returning the card does not undo the −3 ATK / −3 HP on Worm.
- **Hired Hand** — bribe tosses an item. Discard. Return does not undo the Neighbor hit or Flee.
- **Keep cards** (Breastplate, Lantern, Lucky Nickel, Jar o’ Grease, Left/Right Bracer Keep, Shuriken Keep, Iron Chain Keep, Smoke is Use not Keep) — succeeding Keep does not tick. Failing Keep (no bleed on Breastplate, Lantern at 6+ HP, odd Block on Nickel, Zombie blocking Keep) does tick, then may return if Recycler is at 1.
- **Used-up clicks** (Boot, Shield, Ball & Chain dump, most Attacks) — Use, not Discard. After Done, `_discard_spent_items` currently logs those as discard in the combat log. Pitch says Use does not tick. Those leaving after the fight do not drop the number.
- **Challenge full hand / loot throw-away** — Discard. Ticks.
- **Can’t Gain Item** (Zombie Keep-block, gain with a full hand) — the new card never sits. Not a Discard from hand. No tick.
- **Mute Stone** — swap, not Discard.
- **Bag of Holding** — bag emptying at 0 owners tosses the bag. That is a Discard of Recycler’s friend. A bagged Grief Jar coming back from the bag is Bag’s rule, not Recycler.
- **Grief Jar / Sai / Hex / Magnet / Marbles / Bag of Holding closed** — returned copy is drafted fresh (siphon 0, Sai at 2, empty bag, Hex at 6).
- **Toll / Marble / Spider Egg / Blood Pact** — if those can be Discarded, they tick and can be the card that returns.
- **Pacemaker** — also a watcher. Already surplus in Bucket 8. Recycler is the shopping-list hole (B8 T2 Utility, Count + Discard). Two watchers in one bucket until we redistribute leftovers.
- **Ball & Chain** — dumping it is Use. Flee-cancel is not a Discard.
- **Echo Bell** — copying Recycler would copy a snapshot, not a live countdown, unless we say Echo cannot copy a watcher.
- **Two Recyclers** — one Discard, both sitting: both tick, or only one? If both are at 1, does the discarded item come back twice?
- **Recycler itself discarded** — Bell/Cube/Worm eats Recycler. At 3: gone. At 1: is Recycler the item that returns to your Hand?
- **Empty alcove / tutorial** — no Discard. Not in the tutorial kit.
- **Hornet / Queen / Goblin / Commander** — no collision except you still have the item after a Bell tax if Recycler was at 1.

**Generous reading:** Every time a card leaves the hotbar except a successful Keep, it ticks — including used-up items after Done. Two Recyclers both tick. At 1, the item comes back even if the hand is full. Recycler itself can be the card that returns.

**Stingy reading:** Only Bell, Cube, Worm, bribe, Challenge throw, and Keep-fail tick. Used-up items after Done do not. Recycler stays and resets to 5 (it is not discarded). Hand full → Can’t Gain, the item stays gone. Two Recyclers: only the leftmost at 1 returns one copy. Recycler eaten by Bell is just gone, even at 1.

**Ways it can fail:**
- After-fight cleanup currently logs Use as “discard”, so the number would drop on every Boot if we hook the log instead of the rule.
- Keep-fail and Use both call `_toss_item(..., false)` / `_discard_spent_items`. If we cannot tell them apart, Keep-fail would not tick or Boot would.
- Recycler at 1, Bell discards Recycler: infinite return of Recycler, or it eats itself and the countdown never sits.
- Two Recyclers at 1, discard Boot: two Boots, or one Boot and both reset, or one Boot and the second still at 1 (the return is not a Discard).
- Adventure hand of 10 already full, return Can’t Gain — you paid Bell and get nothing.
- Grief Jar returns with its old +Block if we clone the dictionary instead of `_make_item`.
- Returning a card mid-Cube eat while Cube is still waiting for another snack.
- Flashing “1” vs loop icon: Pixel owns art; a loop emoji is not a sprite.

**What the player sees:** A 5 on the card, then 4, 3, 2, then a loud flash instead of 1. Next Discard: that card leaves and immediately sits down again as new. Recycler shows 5.

**If we skip this:** Nothing returns discarded items. Bell, Cube, and Worm are permanent losses. Counting Beads / Hex already count down, but those are Use/Defeat clocks, not Discard.

**If we take the strongest reading:** Two Recyclers plus Bell means you never actually lose a card, and used-up Boots also bounce back every fifth Use. The hotbar cannot empty.

**Questions for Nathan:**
1. When it returns the discarded item, does Recycler stay in the hotbar at 5, or is Recycler also discarded?
2. If Recycler itself is the discarded item while at 1 (Bell/Cube/Worm), does it come back at 5?
3. Two Recyclers both at 1, you Discard Boot: one Boot comes back, or two?
4. Hand is full when the item would return: Can’t Gain (it stays gone), or it sits anyway?

**Self-audit:** restated as what the player does · named Bell/Cube/Worm/Keep-fail/Use-after-fight · both readings · questions would change the code · not implementing this turn

## Answers

1. **Recycler stays** at 5 after it copies. It is unplayable (you never click it).
2. **If Recycler itself is discarded at 1, it comes back at 5.**
3. **Two Recyclers at 1 make two copies.** Ability rewritten to “copied into your Hand.” Grief Jar copies keep their stored Block (copy of that card, not a fresh draft).
4. **Hand full:** the leftover copy is lost (Can't Gain). Recycler still resets. Ordinary one-copy returns fit because you just discarded.

Implemented 2026-10-19. Bucket 8, Tier 2, Utility. Art missing.
