# Paper Fight — Trapper

Implemented 2026-10-28. Card id stays `kobold_trapper`.

**Pitch:** Rename Kobold Trapper to Trapper. 3 HP, 3 ATK. Hunter's Mark. On enter, Mark 2 other monsters. Each Marked Defeat gives +2 HP and +2 ATK.

**What Nathan chose:**

- Every Trapper still on the wheel gains +2 HP and +2 ATK when any Marked monster is Defeated, even a monster that Trapper did not mark.
- A Trapper later in the dungeon, or one who Fled, still grows. A Defeated or Ejected Trapper does not.
- Quiet Bell clears the Marks that Trapper placed. He stops gaining more. HP and ATK he already gained stay.
- Marks do not stack. Each Mark picks an unmarked monster. If none are left, that Mark is lost.
- The word Mark uses the same hover sentence as Marked: upon Defeat, Trapper gains +2 HP and ATK.

**Also settled from the pitch:** Bag of Holding counts. Catapult does not. The +2 is current HP and max HP, and the missing HP stays (1/3 becomes 3/5). The numbers stay after Flee. Phoenix dying counts. The egg is not Marked. Polymorph is not a Defeat, so it does not pay. A Polymorph copy of Trapper keeps the HP and ATK he had already gained, then Marks as a new enter.
