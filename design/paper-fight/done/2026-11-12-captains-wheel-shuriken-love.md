# Paper Fight — Captain's Wheel, Shuriken direction, Love Potion

Resolved 2026-11-12 from Nathan's answers. Implemented in this checkout.

**Captain's Wheel** (id `shardplate`): each Move Left adds 2 Block onto the card. Move Left is the button and every free step in that same direction. Click the card to gain the stored Block and discard it.

**Shuriken:** starts each dungeon pointing Move Right. Flips after every item, including a throw that cannot step. The Ability line, the picture, and the hotbar arrow match. Carries between fights, not between dungeons.

**Love Potion:** purple Defeat. First click must share current HP with another living monster. Second click must match that HP. One of the two dies at random. If they actually die, the other gains that current HP and max HP rises. Lich chips 1 and the potion is Kept. Commander Flees and the gift does not happen. Wraith, Martyr's neighbors, and Phoenix still run when the monster dies, and the lover still receives the snapped HP.
