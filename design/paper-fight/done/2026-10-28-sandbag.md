# Paper Fight — Sandbag

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Pool 4, Tier 1. Sandbag (image: a sentient upright sandbag with eyes, like the Super Smash Bros. Home-Run Contest). 4 HP, 6 Attack. Call name Sandbag. Ability: When you Spin me, if my Attack would kill you, I Flee and lose 1 Attack.

After all On Spin effects end, Sandbag listens for what would happen if the player immediately clicked Take. Retained Block (Stone Wall) can keep that click from being lethal, so Sandbag does not fire. If that click would drop the player to 0 HP, Sandbag flees and loses 1 Attack even when a Healing Potion is in hand.

**Restate (what the player does):**

The wheel lands on Sandbag. Spin abilities finish (poison, and anything else that already changed HP or Attack). Sandbag then pretends the player clicked Take right then, with the Block they already have. If that click would leave them at 0 HP, Sandbag does not hit them. Its Attack goes down by 1, it stays on the wheel, and Flee spins the wheel at random because they are facing it. They never get a chance to play a card in that fight. If the pretend click would leave them at 1 HP or more, nothing special happens: they play the fight and can click Take for real.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

End of the Spin that landed on Sandbag, after On Spin effects, before the item window. No extra click. Sandbag is not used up and is not removed. The Attack loss is on that copy and stays for later Spins. The pretend click uses the Attack and Block at that moment. It is not a snapshot taken when Sandbag entered the dungeon. Closest live card: the Take button’s own damage preview, plus Flee (they stay, and the wheel spins if you are facing them). Healing Potion is the save that must be ignored. Stone Wall is the Block that must count.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- Stone Wall — Block kept from the previous fight is already restored when the Spin lands, before On Spin. The pretend Take sees it. Enough Block to leave you at 1 HP or more means Sandbag stays and you play the fight.
- Healing Potion — a real Take that hits 0 spends the potion and heals 3. This check stops at “HP would become 0” and does not treat the potion as survival. The potion stays in hand because the hit never happens.
- Double Buckler — a real Take halves an even Attack (Sandbag’s 6) before damage, and the Take button already shows that smaller number. Open question whether the pretend click does too.
- Ball and Chain — if anyone would Flee, the Flee does not happen, the banner says Can’t Flee, and the chain’s Attack goes up by 2. Open question whether Sandbag still loses 1 Attack, and whether you then get a normal fight.
- Rust / a piercing hand — a real swing ignores Block. A pretend Take that matches the button would ignore Stone Wall while those are up.
- Poison, Harpy, and other On Spin hits — they already changed your HP before Sandbag listens. If they already dropped you to 0, the fight ended and Sandbag does not listen.
- Flee (keyword) — they are not removed. If you are facing them, the wheel spins at random. Sandbag’s Attack drop happens on the copy that stays.
- A wheel with only Sandbag left — Flee can only land on Sandbag again. Open question whether that new landing listens again.
- Bodyguard — takes a neighbor’s Attack, not the player’s. It does not step in front of this hit.
- Hornet, Shambling Mound — extra swings are their rule, not Sandbag’s. Sandbag is one swing.
- Goblin — cannot take Attack damage. That does not change damage dealt to the player.
- Wraith — copies a monster’s Attack. If Sandbag’s number actually dropped, a later copy sees the new number.
- Silence — same as other monster text: a silenced Sandbag does not listen. You just Take the hit.
- Tutorial / Developer Test — Sandbag is not in the tutorial kit. A test seat can spin it.
- Empty seat — Flee does not remove Sandbag, so it does not open a hole.

**Generous reading:**

The pretend click is the real Take pipeline: Block, Rust, pierce, and Double Buckler. Only the potion (the save after 0) is ignored. You lose no HP. Attack drops by 1, then Flee spins. If that spin lands on Sandbag and the new Attack would still kill you, it flees again until a landing would leave you alive. Ball and Chain stops the spin, Sandbag still loses 1 Attack, and you get the normal fight so you can play cards before Take. Take uses the new Attack.

**Stingy reading:**

“My Attack” means Sandbag’s Attack minus Block only. A Double Buckler in hand does not stop the flee, even if the button would have said a smaller number. Ball and Chain cancels the whole reaction: Attack stays 6, Flee does not happen, and you are in a normal fight against the lethal hit. A Flee spin that lands on Sandbag again does not listen; you face the lethal Take on that second landing.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**
- If every new landing listens, and something puts the Attack back up as fast as it falls, the wheel never stops spinning.
- If Attack is allowed to go below 0, later math lies. The drop stops at 0 unless you say otherwise.
- If the flee succeeds but the hit also lands, the Ability’s “would” and the potion note both lie.
- If Chain cancels Flee and the Attack drop already happened, the Take button must show the new number or the player cannot tell what they are about to click.
- Solo Sandbag: repeated flee-spins until the hit is no longer lethal, then a normal fight. That is a long animation, not a lock, as long as each success lowers Attack and 0 Attack cannot kill.

**What the player sees:**

Spin lands on the sandbag. If the hit would drop them to 0, the sandbag does not swing. Its Attack number ticks down by 1. The wheel spins itself. If the hit would leave them standing, the fight opens as usual and the Take button is the real click.

**If we skip this:**

No monster looks at the Take button and leaves before the hit. Cowardly King (now Petty King) does something else. Flee still only means “spin if you are facing them; they stay.”

**If we take the strongest reading:**

One Spin onto a lethal Sandbag can chain several random spins, dropping Attack each time, until the number no longer kills. You never play a card in those skipped landings. A Buckler or carried Block can stop the chain on the first look. Ball and Chain turns the skip into a normal fight at the lower Attack.
