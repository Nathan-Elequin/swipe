# Paper Fight — Vampire

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**
Tier 3. Bucket 1. 4 HP, 4 Attack. Call name Bloodlust. “Every time you lose HP, I gain +1 HP and +1 ATK.” It updates when the player’s HP changes.

**Chosen reading:**
A hit or a bleed that actually drops the heart number gives every living Vampire on the wheel +1 HP and +1 Attack, once. A 4-heart hit is still +1/+1. The extras stay until that Vampire dies or leaves. Quiet Bell stops further gains and keeps the HP and Attack already gained.

**Where it happens:**
No click. Same moments Ogre listens: a swing that gets through Block, a bleed, Needleback’s poke. The new Attack is for the next swing.
