# Paper Fight — Lucky Nickel

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Rework Lucky Nickel. Ability: Gain 2 Block, or Gain 6 Block and Lose 2 HP. Keep. 50% odds between the two. Keep no matter the outcome.

**Restate (what the player does):**

During a fight, you click Lucky Nickel. You do not click a monster, and you do not pick which result you want. The game flips a coin. Half the time you gain 2 Block. Half the time you gain 6 Block and then lose 2 HP. Either way the card is spent for this fight and comes back for later fights. The Block number is added once. It does not keep changing after the flip. The Block itself still clears at the end of the fight.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

Fight, your item click, before you press Done. No target. One flip per click. Keep means the same thing it means on Lantern: gone for the rest of this fight, still in your hand next fight. You cannot click it again this fight. The 2 or the 6 is one Block gain. Lose 2 HP is one loss of exactly 2, the same phrase as Arrowhead. Block does not stop that loss. It still happens if the Block gain already Defeated the enemy. Healing Potion can still save you if that loss would drop you to 0.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- Arrowhead — "and Lose 2 HP" already means: do the first part, then lose 2 HP directly. Spiked Flail and Pacemaker see that loss. You still lose the 2 if the first part Defeated them.
- Pacemaker — a loss of exactly 2 HP heals 1 while Pacemaker is in hand. The bad flip is that loss.
- Spiked Flail — one HP-loss event, so it gains +2 Attack once, not twice.
- Hollow — while you face Hollow, an even Block gain happens twice. 2 becomes two gains of 2. 6 becomes two gains of 6. Quiet Bell turns that off.
- Eel — while you face the Eel, the Block you gain hits the Eel as Attack for that much. If Hollow doubled the gain, the Eel takes the doubled hit. If that Defeats the Eel on the bad flip, you still lose 2 HP.
- Hydra — on Spin, petrifies every item whose live Attack or Block number is even. Nathan chose: this card counts as even Block, so Hydra petrifies it.
- Basilisk — first Spin can petrify items in even hand slots (2nd, 4th, …). That is about the seat, not this card's numbers. A petrified nickel cannot be clicked.
- Gargoyle — petrifies by Attack rank. This card is Block, so Gargoyle does not pick it for that.
- Leech League — when you lose HP, it loses that much HP. Anvil still cannot lose more than 1 HP at a time.
- Vampire — when you lose HP, it gains +1 HP and +1 Attack.
- Dragon — Attack ignores Block. The Block from this card does not stop a pierce hit.
- Rust — monsters ignore Block while it is up. You still gain the Block number.
- Hornet and Queen — the Block sits in the normal pile. Hornet's two swings share it.
- Goblin — ignores Attack damage. This card does not Attack the monster you face, except the Eel case above.
- Healing Potion — can save a loss that would drop you to 0.
- Decay — if this card is linked, using it still Keeps it, and the linked card is discarded.
- Amalgam — the click counts as an item use.
- Empty seat — you cannot play it if you are not in a fight.

**Generous reading:**

Both coin results Keep. The bad result pays 2 HP that your new Block does not absorb. Hydra does not petrify this card.

**Stingy reading:**

Both coin results Keep. The bad result still pays 2 HP that Block does not absorb. Hydra petrifies it.

**Answer:**

Petrify it until the next spin. Both numbers are even Block.

**What the player sees:**

The card says Gain 2 Block, or Gain 6 Block and Lose 2 HP. Keep. The hotbar mark reads 2/6. Hold Shift says 50% (2) or (6 and Lose 2 HP). Keep. You click once. Block goes up by 2, or Block goes up by 6 and your HP drops by 2. The card leaves play for this fight and is back next fight.
