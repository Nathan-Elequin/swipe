# Paper Fight — Pocket Watch attacks Across

Implemented this chat.

**Chosen reading:**

You may hypnotize any living monster, including the one you are facing. They use the current Pocket Watch attack (snapshot Attack, extra swings, Damsel’s neighbors then Heal 3 on the victim, Dire Wolf’s number, Needleback back at the hypnotized monster, Goblin takes 0, Bodyguard can move the hit, your Block does not reduce it). The victim is the living monster halfway around the other living monsters. Empty corridors are skipped, so Across is always a living monster when the count is even. An odd living-monster count makes the card unusable. Nathan said an empty wheel is not a state where items can be used. That is why Across walks living monsters only.

**Ability:** Target any enemy. That enemy attacks the monster that is Across from them.

**Across tooltip:** across on the opposite side of the wheel.

**Explainer:** Pick any monster. They Attack the monster Across from them. Unplayable when the number of monsters is odd.

**Portrait:** The facing monster stays on the wheel. During the swing, the right portrait shows the victim. The hypnotized monster is on the left. If the victim is not the one you were facing, the facing portrait steps aside and comes back after.

**Telegraph:** After Pocket Watch is clicked, hovering a monster gives them a gold rim and draws a rippling red arrow to the monster Across from them, with a red glow on that monster.
