# Paper Fight — Glass Asp rework

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Rework Glass Asp to 12 HP and 2 ATK. Call name stays Bitter Blood. Ability: On Spin, I Gain 2 Poison. When I lose HP, I gain equal Attack. The slash portrait, animation, and sound should work almost exactly like Ogre, except the slash plays when Glass Asp loses HP and Glass Asp gains Attack.

**Chosen reading (2026-11-12):**

- Every Spin adds 2 Poison and does not clear it. It ticks only when you are facing that Asp, like other Poison.
- Any HP it actually loses counts, including that Poison tick.
- Attack goes up by the HP that left the bar. A 10-damage hit into 3 HP left adds 3. Healing does not remove the Attack.
- The Ogre-style slash plays only when Attack goes up. Gaining Poison does not slash.

**Collisions written down while building:**

- Attack damage and other HP loss use two paths (`_deal_atk_at` and `_chip_hp`). Both call the same helper.
- Fairy can put a point of HP back before the count, so that saved point does not add Attack.
- Hydra’s eat Defeats a neighbor without subtracting the remaining HP, so that remaining HP does not add Attack.
- There was no Glass Asp ability sound. The slash uses Ogre’s ability sound.
- A big Poison tick flies one Attack pip per point, the same way Ogre flies one heart per point.
