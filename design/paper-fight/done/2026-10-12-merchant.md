# Paper Fight — Merchant

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

New Monster. Tier 1. Bucket 3–5. Merchant. 4 Attack, 4 HP. Call name Fair Trade. “When you Spin me, choose an Item and I will upgrade it.”

After Spin, and before items can be used, show an interface similar to Gelatinous Cube and require the player to choose an item. That item is immediately re-rolled into one of a higher tier. If they choose a Tier 3, it simply becomes another Tier 3 item. If the player has none, nothing happens. Set Spider Egg to be a Tier 0 item, so it can be turned into a Tier 1 item.

**Bucket:** He gave 3–5 and said to choose. Use **bucket 4**. Do not ask. Bucket 4 already has four monsters (Mimic, Needleback, Clockwork Knight, Beggar) and three items (Spiked Shield, Lantern, Mirror Shard). Merchant makes five monsters there, so the editor warning will fire. That does not change the recommendation.

**Restate (what the player does):**

The wheel lands on Merchant. He shows 4 Attack and 4 HP. If the hand is empty, nothing extra happens and the fight is normal. If the hand has items, buttons appear and the player must click one before any item or Done. That card is replaced at once by a different tier: Tier 0 becomes Tier 1, Tier 1 becomes Tier 2, Tier 2 becomes Tier 3, and Tier 3 stays Tier 3. Merchant is not used up. When the player later presses Done, he still attacks once for 4.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

Same moment as Gelatinous Cube: the spin has landed, Cursed Scroll and the siphon jars have already had their chance to hurt you, and item clicks are not open yet. Merchant stays on the wheel. The player must click an item. The new card is a fresh item. It does not keep a running copy of the old card’s number. Empty hand: skip the buttons and fight normally.

The replacement sits in the same hand slot, the way Chaos Stone rerolls a card. It is not a Gain, so it does not go through the “new item enters the hand” path.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- Gelatinous Cube. Same lock. Zero items: nothing. One item: one button, and you still have to click it. You cannot skip. Done stays locked, and item clicks do nothing, until a button is pressed. The cube’s own buttons offer at most two random items and then discard the one you pick. That discard is not an item use. Merchant must not reuse those Feed buttons, or the item will be thrown away instead of replaced.
- Chaos Stone. Click the stone, then click other items. Each chosen item is replaced by a random catalog item of the same tier. Retired and hold cards are skipped. Todo cards are included. The bucket does not matter. The copy is not removed from this dungeon’s draft pile. The same item can come back. The card keeps its place in the hand. Merchant is that replacement, on spin, one tier higher, and the stone is not spent because Merchant is the monster.
- Spider Egg. Queen creates it. It is not drafted. Bucket is Token. Count is 0. Tier is 1 today. When we build, set tier to 0 so Merchant can turn it into a Tier 1 item. See the lock note below. The upgrade math has to read that stored 0. The helper that paints tier metal treats anything under 1 as 1, so a Tier 0 egg would become a Tier 2 item if we use that helper.
- Spider Queen. Eggs already in hand when the wheel lands can be chosen, and an upgraded egg is no longer an egg, so she will not eat it later. She lays a new egg on this spin after the cube builds its offer and before the player can click. Whether that new egg is one of Merchant’s buttons is a question below. If the faced monster is the Queen, she eats eggs on that spin. Merchant’s upgrade does not run then.
- Basilisk. Only while you face Basilisk. This spin faces Merchant, so his “cannot Gain or Keep” does not apply. Chaos Stone’s replacement is also not a Gain. The upgrade still happens. Not a question.
- Quiet Bell. Silence lasts for the rest of this dungeon, by monster id. If Merchant was already silenced before this spin, skip the buttons, same as a silenced cube. The bell cannot be clicked before the choice, because items are locked until you pick. Not a question.
- Gargoyle. Petrify is cleared at the start of this spin, before the choice. The item you upgrade is not petrified. Gargoyle’s lock does not carry into this fight. Not a question.
- Echo Bell. The swap is not an item use, same as feeding the cube, so Echo does not update to the new card. If the card you upgrade is Echo Bell itself, the copy it was showing is wiped and the new item is whatever was drawn.
- Beggar. His Attack is the leftmost Attack number in hand, and it is refreshed when the hand changes. After the swap, he reads the new card.
- Harpy and Kobold. No extra poke and no extra swing. This is not an item use.
- Hornet. Merchant does not swing twice. One Attack of 4 when you press Done.
- Wraith. Copies another owner’s Attack. Merchant’s Attack stays 4. The item swap does not change that.
- Commander and Lich. The faced monster’s numbers are locked for the fight. Neighbor bonuses do not change Merchant’s 4 Attack or 4 HP while you fight him. Same as other faced monsters.
- Dragon. Merchant does not pierce.
- Goblin. Merchant’s Attack does hurt you.
- Mimic. No gift. He attacks unless you kill him with items after the upgrade.
- Slime. Attack is 4, not equal to HP.
- Empty alcove, last owner. No extra rule.
- Tutorial kit. Merchant is not in those seats. Do not put him there. A forced button would block the scripted Done.
- Developer Test. Seating him still runs the choice.
- Cursed Scroll and siphon jars. They already run before the cube asks. If they drop you to 0 HP, the fight ends and Merchant never asks.

**Spider Egg tier, when we build:**

Set `spider_egg` tier from 1 to 0 on the board and in the catalog. Leave the Token bucket, the 0 count, and “Queen creates it.” Do not draft it.

The tier field is **not** author-locked. `author_lock` is false, `locked_fields` is empty, `locked_at` is empty, and `item:spider_egg` is not in `data/author-locks.json`. There is no locked key to override.

**Generous reading:**

Every item in the hand is a button, including the egg Queen lays on this same spin. The new item can be any live card of the destination tier, including a bucket this run has not opened. A Tier 3 item can come back as a different Tier 3 item, or as itself if that reading is the one he picks. You still have to click the one button when you only hold one item.

**Stingy reading:**

Same lock as the cube: at most two random items, chosen when the wheel lands, before Queen lays. The new item comes only from what is still left in this dungeon’s draft pile, and that copy is used up. A Tier 3 item must become a different Tier 3 item. One item is still a forced click, not an automatic upgrade.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**

- Reusing the cube’s Feed handler discards the item and does not upgrade it.
- Reading tier through the metal helper turns a Tier 0 egg into a Tier 2 item.
- If the destination pile is empty, the buttons must still go away and the old item must stay. Otherwise Done is locked forever.
- If the chosen card leaves the hand before the click lands, the click must do nothing and close the prompt, not stick.
- A tutorial seat with this prompt blocks the scripted Done.
- The player can roll a todo card if we copy Chaos Stone’s pile exactly. Several unfinished items are still todo, including Tier 3 Breastplate, Healing Potion, and Pocketwatch.
- Upgrading Echo Bell mid-copy makes the card change identity under the echo icon. That matches “this card becomes another item.” The echo memory does not move to the new card.
