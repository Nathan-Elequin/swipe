# Paper Fight — Cleric

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

New monster. Tier 2. 5 HP, 4 Attack. Name Cleric. Call name Health Insurance. “Whenever you Bleed while I am in the Dungeon, I will Heal you for 2 and then take your worst item.”

On any HP loss, at any time, show the Cleric at their seat with a glowing-light aura (about 0.7s). That light moves to your hearts and Heals 2. The same light then moves to an item in the hotbar and deletes it. Worst item is lowest tier; ties are random. Spider Egg is Tier 0, so it is taken first.

If you lose HP several times in a combo (Damsel’s neighbors, Hornet Swarm), let that series finish, count how many times HP dropped, then play the Cleric that many times with no click in between. If the first hit of a multiattack would kill you, you die and nothing happens. If only the second hit would kill you, you still die, because Cleric waits until the whole sequence is done.

**Restate (what the player does):**

You do not click Cleric. While any Cleric is still on the wheel, every time your HP number goes down and you are still alive after that whole burst of hits, they Heal 2 and delete your lowest-tier item. Hornet’s two swings, Damsel’s two neighbors, and Kobold’s extra swings wait until the last swing. Then the light plays once per counted loss. A blocked swing is not a loss. One 4-damage swing that you survive is one loss. If that burst drops you to 0 and Healing Potion does not save you, you die and Cleric does nothing.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

Any time a living unsilenced Cleric is on the wheel: Spin taxes, fights, side pokes, leftover chips from other seats. No click. Not used up. The Heal is always 2, capped at 6. The stolen card is whichever living hand card has the lowest tier at that moment. Quiet Bell on that Cleric turns this off.

Bucket: he did not give a range. **Bucket 6** with the other Tier 2 ability monsters (Gargoyle, Harpy, Kobold). That bucket goes from 3 monsters to 4.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- **Hornet Swarm / Kobold Trapper** — extra swings you cannot click between. You said wait, count each swing that actually dropped HP, then Cleric that many times. If swing 1 kills you, you die and Cleric never plays. If swing 1 leaves you at 1 and swing 2 kills you, you die and Cleric never plays. Healing Potion can still save you on a lethal swing the way it already does; if it does, you are not dead, the rest of the swings still happen, then Cleric counts every loss in that burst including the one Potion reversed.
- **Damsel** — left neighbor then right neighbor, each may be a Hornet. That whole neighbor string is one sequence. Then Damsel Heals you 3 and leaves. Cleric after the neighbor hits, before or after Damsel’s Heal 3, changes whether you sit at 6 or steal on a full bar. Leftover question if both fit; I am writing Cleric after the neighbor hits and before Damsel’s Heal 3, so a 4+4 that you survive is two Cleric plays, then Heal 3.
- **Dragon** — pierce. A hit that gets through is one loss.
- **Cursed Scroll / Grief Jar** — On Spin you lose 1 (each). You cannot click between them. Whether they batch with Harpy’s meet-hit is a leftover question.
- **Spider Egg** — click loss is one event. It is also Tier 0, so it is the first item stolen.
- **Needleback** — poke 1 after Attack damage. That poke is HP loss if Block does not stop it.
- **Harpy** — meet-hit on Spin, and again after each item. Each is a swing you cannot click during.
- **Healing Potion** — saves you at 0 in the middle of a burst. You said Cleric does not save you. Potion still can. After the burst, if you lived, Cleric counts the losses.
- **Vampire / Ogre / Dire Wolf / Bloody Wraps / Spiked Flail / Breastplate** — they listen when HP drops, during the burst, before Cleric’s delayed Heal. Ogre already lost HP. Vampire already grew. Then you Heal 2. Wolf follows the healed number after Cleric.
- **Mimic / Clockwork / Commander / King** — leave rules do not replace this. A Cleric killed by Attack or Defeat is gone and stops insuring. A Cleric who is Damsel’s neighbor swings and stays, so they still insure later.
- **Da Boot / Fangs / Sledgehammer / Paci-Fist** — Defeat the Cleric, no more insurance. Fangs makes you take 4 first; that 4 is a one-swing sequence, then they die. If that 4 kills you, no Cleric (they are about to leave anyway). If you live, one Heal 2 and a steal, then Fangs defeats them.
- **Quiet Bell** — unique ability off. No Heal, no steal from that Cleric.
- **Basilisk** — blocks Gain and Keep. Cleric deletes an item. That is not Gain or Keep.
- **Gargoyle** — a petrified card can still be the worst item and can still be stolen.
- **Gelatinous Cube / Merchant / Chaos Stone** — other ways items leave. Cube feed is not HP loss. Merchant can upgrade the egg so it is no longer Tier 0.
- **Counting Beads / Sai / Wick / Kobold** — steal is not an item play. They do not tick.
- **Echo Bell / Lantern / Knife Keep** — stolen means gone. Keep does not bring it back.
- **Goblin / Slime / Wraith / Lich / Skeleton / Anvil / Bodyguard** — no special collision on the insurance. Bodyguard does not stop you losing HP.
- **Empty hand / last item / tutorial / Developer Test** — last item can be stolen. Empty hand is a leftover question. Tutorial does not seat Cleric. Developer Test can.

**Generous reading:**

Empty hand: still Heal 2. Two Clerics both pay 2 and each steal on every counted loss. Cleric’s own Done hit still insures you before they leave. Spin taxes, Harpy meet, item + Needleback + Harpy poke all wait, then a stack of lights. Potion can save a lethal first Hornet swing, then Cleric refunds 2 per loss afterward.

**Stingy reading:**

Empty hand: no Heal, because there is no worst item. Two Clerics: only one of them pays. Their own Done: they leave first, that 4 does not insure. Only Hornet / Kobold / Damsel bursts wait; Scroll, Grief, Egg, Needleback, and Harpy each fire Cleric immediately after that one loss. Lethal burst: you die, no light.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**

- Cleric Heals in the middle of Hornet and you live a second swing you were supposed to die to.
- First Hornet swing kills you, Potion saves you, Cleric is skipped because “would kill.”
- Sequence never ends (Damsel neighbor is Damsel) and the Heal queue never plays.
- Steal runs with an empty hand and deletes a missing card, or Heal is skipped when you still wanted the 2.
- Two Clerics steal the same item, or steal while Cube is asking for a feed.
- Light plays from a seat after Crossbow already removed that Cleric.
- Shift line says Bleed and a blocked hit still pays, or an Attack chip does not.
- Tutorial never seats them, so that path is safe.

Every Scroll tick, every Hornet chip, every Damsel neighbor is Heal 2 and your worst card disappears. Two Clerics double it. A live Cleric makes Cursed Scroll a Heal 1 and a tax on the egg. That can empty a hand in one Damsel Done, or keep you topped up for the rest of the dungeon until they die.
