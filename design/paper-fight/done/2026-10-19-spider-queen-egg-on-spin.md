# Paper Fight — Spider Queen Egg on Spin

**Pitch:** Each time you Spin, Gain 1 Spider Egg. Spinning the Queen discards those eggs; she gains +2 HP and +2 Attack for each.

**Restate:** Any Spin while a Queen is alive puts an Egg in your hand (if a slot is free). If that Spin landed on her, she then eats every Egg you hold.

**Nathan’s answer (2026-10-19):** Generous. Spinning her with no Eggs still Grants 1, then she eats it: +2 HP and +2 Attack.

**If we skip this:** Spinning her with an empty Egg pile does nothing. Eggs only appear from a Queen in another seat.
