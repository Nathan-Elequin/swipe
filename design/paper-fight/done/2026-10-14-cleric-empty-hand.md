# Paper Fight — Cleric empty-hand deny

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Small change to Cleric with the ability Health Insurance. If the player has zero items remaining, and they also are missing at least three health, the Cleric should initially begin and have the text Capsule Fire off to show that they're intending to heal us, but then they should have a custom dialogue box custom dialogue speech bubble that appears over their sprite that says, "Wait, you don't have anything to pay. No healing for you." And then we'll deny the healing with some sort of fun animation to indicate that we don't get any healing from that ability.

**Restate (what the player does):**

You do not click Cleric. After a Spin, once the other automatic On Spin cards have already run, if a living unsilenced Cleric is on the wheel and you are missing at least 3 HP, they still start Health Insurance the way they do when they will pay: the Health Insurance capsule fires from their seat toward your hearts. If your hotbar is empty at that moment, they do not Heal 3. A speech bubble appears over their sprite: “Wait, you don't have anything to pay. No healing for you.” Then a cancel motion shows that no hearts came back. If you still hold any item, Heal 3 and take the worst item as they already do.

**Where it happens in a round:** After Spin, after Cursed Scroll, Grief Jar, Harpy meet, poison, leech, Queen eggs, and Juggernaut. No click. Not used up. Quiet Bell on that Cleric still turns this off. Take, Done, and fight chips still do not trigger this. Merchant or Cube may still ask after.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- **Queen / Juggernaut** — egg or Shield lands first. If that put something in your hand, Cleric pays and takes it. Empty-hand deny only if the hotbar is still empty after those gifts.
- **Cursed Scroll / Grief Jar / Harpy meet** — those chips land first. If they leave you at 3 HP or less and alive, Cleric starts. Empty hand: tease, then 0 Heal.
- **Two Clerics** — first one on an empty hand does not Heal, so you are still missing at least 3. Second Cleric would also see empty hand. Collision: both tease-deny, or only the first.
- **Dire Wolf / Ogre / Vampire / Bloody Wraps / Spiked Flail / Breastplate** — no Heal means their numbers stay on the unhealed HP.
- **Healing Potion** — still the only save if Spin taxes would kill you. If you die, Cleric never starts.
- **Quiet Bell** — that Cleric does not start, no capsule, no bubble.
- **Merchant / Cube** — still after Cleric. Deny does not skip their ask.
- **Hornet / Kobold / Damsel / Needleback / Fangs** — still not a Spin. Unchanged.
- **Basilisk** — deny deletes nothing, so Keep/Gain does not come up.
- **Goblin / Dragon / Mimic / Slime / Wraith / Commander / King / tutorial** — tutorial does not seat Cleric. The rest do not change the empty-hand check.
- **Developer Test** — can seat Cleric with an empty hotbar to see this.

**Generous reading:** Capsule fires as if they will pay. Speech. Deny with a cancel motion. HP stays. Two Clerics: only the first speaks; the second stays quiet because the joke already landed. Locked Ability text stays; only Flow/Notes change.

**Stingy reading:** Capsule fires. Speech. No Heal. Two Clerics both play the full tease-and-deny. Ability, Flow, and Notes all get rewritten so inspect mentions that no item means no Heal.

**Nathan's answers:**
1. Both Clerics play the full tease-and-deny (capsule, speech, no Heal).
2. Yes — change Flow and Notes so empty hand does not Heal. Ability / Call name / explainer stay as saved.
