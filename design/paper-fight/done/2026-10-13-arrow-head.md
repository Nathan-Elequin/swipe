The unanswered write-up from this fight is below.

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

New item. Arrow Head. Art of an arrow head, sharp. Tier 2, Bucket 3–5. Ability: “Atk 3. While in hand, your Attacks deal +1 Dmg and Enemy Attacks ignore your Block.”

Can be used as a normal ATK 3 weapon. While it is in hand, every item in hand that deals Attack damage gets +1 after every other calculation (Counting Beads can hit 5). That +1 also applies to Spider Egg even though Egg hits you. While it is in hand, every enemy attack works like Dragon: you can still gain Block, but Block is ignored when figuring and resolving enemy attacks of every kind, including Needleback thorns, Damsel’s neighbors, and anything else that swings at you. Take X / Done must use that math. The card needs static icons for both passives, not only “Atk 3”, and a clear persistent animation that this card is the reason Block is being ignored.

**Restate (what the player does):**

During a fight you click Arrow Head. It deals 4 Attack (3 + 1 for this copy). The card is used up. You do not pick a second target.

As long as any unspent copy is still in hand (petrified still counts):

1. Every item Attack number gets +1 after that item’s own formula (Beads 4 becomes 5).
2. Every enemy attack ignores Block the way Dragon does. Block stays; it is not eaten.

Hovering Done / Take X shows the full hit through Block. Spending Arrow Head turns both effects off before Harpy’s poke.

**Where it happens in a round:** Fight only. Used up when clicked. No extra target. The +1 and ignore-Block keep updating while copies are in hand.

Bucket: **4**. Count 1.

## Nathan's answers

1. Clicking it deals **4**.
2. After that click it is **gone** — Block can stop Harpy’s poke.
3. Two copies: **+1 per copy**.
4. Spider Egg: **Take 3, Block still works**.
5. Gargoyle petrifies it: **aura stays**.
