# Paper Fight — Phantom rework

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Buff stats to 5/5. Ability: “When you use a non-Shrouded Item, I Shroud one of your Items.” Shroud one item, not two. Shrouded items do not become revealed when Phantom is defeated. Shroud stays until the next time that item is used. A Keep item that is used still loses Shroud, and it does not become Shrouded again unless Phantom’s ability hits it later.

**Restate (what the player does):** Phantom is on the wheel at 5 HP and 5 ATK. Call name stays Dark Matter. When you use an item that is not Shrouded, and a living Phantom is not silenced, Phantom shrouds one other item in your hand and slides it to the right end. You do not click a target. The shrouded item stays hidden until you use it. Using it peels the veil and then plays the item. That use does not shroud anything else. If Phantom dies, the veil stays. A Keep you use comes back clean. A later non-Shrouded use can shroud it again.

**Where it happens in a round:** During the fight, after you use a non-Shrouded item. The item you just used is not the one that gets shrouded. The shroud flag stays on that item until you use it, including after Phantom is defeated and in a later fight if the item is still in hand.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- Phantom / Dark Matter — was shroud-and-shuffle 2 until Defeat, then unshroud all.
- Keep items — use clears Shroud; the same use does not put Shroud back on it.
- Unplayable shrouded click — still reveals, then discards, with no item effect.
- Quiet Bell — still turns the trigger off. It does not clear veils that are already on items.

**Generous reading:** The new sentence drops the move. The item stays in its slot.

**Stingy reading:** The item still slides to the right end.

**Nathan’s answer:** Slide to the right end, the way the old shuffle did.

**Built:** HP 5 (ATK was already 5). Ability is his sentence. One random other item. Defeat does not clear Shroud. Use does, including Keep.
