# Paper Fight — Danger Magnet

Answers in. Shipped.

**Chosen reading:**

Not an Attack card. Three states: Highest Attack, Lowest Attack, Off. Click at Ready or during a fight (Net/Cube pills). Highest or Lowest arms this copy. Off clears it. The next Spin of any kind (button, auto-spin, Smoke, Commander, Wick, Vampire shuffle) lands on a living monster with that Attack extreme; ties pick at random. Then this copy is discarded. Tutorial scripted landings ignore it and do not discard it. Two copies: most recently set Highest or Lowest wins; copies armed that way are discarded.

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Danger Magnet. Not ATK 3 (that was Arrow Head). Click: modal like Net / Cube. Three toggleable states: Highest Attack, Lowest Attack, Off. It stays until the next targeted Spin, then deletes itself. Controls the random Spin: that Spin lands on the monster with Greatest or Least Attack. Ties: random among those Attack values. Set it during a fight and at Ready (first Spin of a dungeon). Every time the wheel actually spins while armed, including shuffles. Tutorial still forces its scripted landings.

**Restate (what the player does):**

Click Danger Magnet at Ready or in a fight. Three pills: Highest Attack, Lowest Attack, Off. Highest or Lowest marks the card. Off un-marks it. You do not click a monster. The next wheel Spin stops on a living monster whose Attack is highest or lowest (read at Spin, after formulas). Then the Magnet leaves your hand. Until you pick Highest or Lowest, Spin stays random and the card stays.

**Where it happens in a round:** Ready and fight. Targeting keeps the card until that Spin, then it is discarded. Attack ranking is read at Spin.

**Questions answered:**
1. Duration — next targeted Spin, then discard.

3. When — Ready and fight.
4. Which spins — all real spins including shuffles. Tutorial scripted landings still win.
