# Paper Fight — Flee, 0 HP removal, unplayable hatch

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Flee interrupts combat at the end of the current action, then instantly spins the wheel at random in every mode. 0 HP units are always removed and the gap is filled before that spin. Unplayable items get a uniform hatch overlay that stays readable (Toll, Sledgehammer, The Boot).

**Restate (what the player does):** They use an item or take a monster’s attack. That action finishes, including knock-ons (Needleback, Bodyguard taking the hit). Anyone at 0 HP leaves and the wheel closes up / fills. If the action said Flee and the intended enemy still has HP, combat ends immediately and the wheel spins to a random living monster. They do not pick.

**Where it happens in a round:** End of the current item or the monster’s resolved attack. Dead units are used up and gone. Flee does not wait for Done. The spin is random, copied once for that spin (not a lasting magnet). No extra click.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- **Shuriken** — 2 Attack, then Flee and Keep if the facing enemy lives. Bodyguard can eat that hit and die; facing still lives, so Shuriken still Flees after Bodyguard is removed.
- **Bodyguard** — takes a neighbor’s Attack. At 0 HP they leave like any other death, including during Flee.
- **Needleback / similar on-hit** — knock-ons resolve before Flee.
- **Commander** — first time they would die they Flee instead of leaving.
- **Cowardly King** — Flees after their own attack; same random spin.
- **Goblin** — immune to Attack, so Shuriken’s facing target lives and you Flee.
- **Hornet / Queen** — extra swings are their attack; Flee after that action, not mid-swing, unless an item Flees first.
- **Wraith / Slime / Dragon** — numbers still apply to the hit that happened; Flee does not copy them.
- **Empty seat / Challenge hole** — death fills the seat the same way as a Crossbow kill, then Flee spins.
- **Tutorial** — existing tutorial spin forcing stays; Flee in tutorial still does not auto-start a new fight if the tutorial already blocks auto-spin.
- **Developer Test** — random among remaining live seats.

**Generous reading:** Flee always random-spins in Challenge too; every 0 HP seat is removed and filled before that spin, even when the kill was a neighbor.

**Stingy reading:** Challenge Flee used to wait for a click and could leave a 0 HP Bodyguard on the wheel. That is not this rule.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**
- Flee spinning before the dead neighbor is removed (the Shuriken + Bodyguard case).
- Challenge still waiting for a click after Flee.
- Toll / Hex hashed so hard the countdown cannot be read.
- Sledgehammer still looking live when the facing Attack is under 6.

**What the player sees:** Unplayable hotbar cards get a hatch, not a grey plate. After Shuriken, a dead Bodyguard is gone, then the wheel spins by itself.

**If we skip this:** Shuriken + Bodyguard stays stuck at 0 HP; Challenge Flee still asks them to click.

**If we take the strongest reading:** One Shuriken can delete a neighbor, fill that seat, and throw them into a random new fight with no Done click. That is the intended Flee.
