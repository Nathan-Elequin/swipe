# Paper Fight — Lich Undying, Fairy Dust

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Lich, Pool 3, Tier 2, 2 HP, 5 Attack. Call name Undying. Ability: When you use Defeat Items against me, they fail, reduce my HP by 1, and are Kept. I cannot lose HP in any other way.

Defeat items still have to be legally usable. On use they are tapped like any other Kept item, and Lich loses 1 HP. Lich is still defeated as normal when they attack at the end of combat. Hex stays in the inventory as Kept, and its counter resets to 6.

The old Lich neighbor aura moves onto Fairy. Archive the Fairy in a Bottle grant.

**Answers:**

1. Only The Boot, Sledgehammer, Fangs, Paci-Fist, Scythe, and Hex.
2. Fangs still makes Lich attack. Heal uses the HP from before the chip. A fresh Lich is Heal 2.
3. Each Hex chips 1. Two Hexes take Lich from 2 to 0 before they attack. Both reset to 6 and stay.
4. Quiet Bell turns Undying off.
5. Fairy uses 1 HP, 3 Attack, two copies, call name Fairy Dust. The neighbor aura moves with her. The bottle is no longer created.

**Built:**

Those six items chip 1 and are Kept when Lich is not silenced. Echo Bell’s copy is still used up. Other HP loss does not move Lich. If HP remains, the end-of-combat attack still removes Lich. Fairy paints +1 current and max HP on living neighbors. Pool 3 now has six monsters. No card was dragged out.
