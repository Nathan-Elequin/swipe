# Paper Fight — Trapper rework (Bear Trap) — done 2026-10-30

Implemented after Nathan’s answers. Sling Paper Fight stayed in CURRENT.md.

**Pitch:** Trapper T2 2 HP 3 ATK, Call Bear Trap: When I arrive, I place a Bear Trap on top of the Dungeon Deck. Token Bear Trap 2 HP 4 ATK: When you Spin me or Move onto me, I immediately Attack you and then I am Defeated. Move onto trap activates. Jar first slide onto trap cancels the second.

**Answers:**
1. Arrive = Trapper sits on the wheel.
2. Trap never fills without a Spin (not on opening wheel; hole fill into a just-cleared seat still Spins).
3. Any Spin or movement. Shuriken fully resolves first (its 3 can Defeat the trap so it never Attacks). Jar first-seat snap still cancels the second slide.
4. Block works, then the trap is still Defeated.
5. Ordinary monster Defeat (draw from the Dungeon Deck / hole fill).
6. Quiet Bell does not stop Trapper placing the trap (already fired on spawn).
7. Hover: “a 2 HP 4 ATK monster that Attacks you when you Move or Spin onto it”

**Tabled (picked while coding, no second question round):**
- Cleric / Queen eggs / other after-Spin effects: skipped if the trap snaps on land and the fight leaves that seat (1 theme: on-spin tail after an immediate self-Defeat).
- Quiet Bell on a seated trap: snap still happens (Nathan: Quiet Bell is irrelevant for this card).
- Bodyguard eating Shuriken’s 3: trap can still be alive and then snap.
- Hook walking through a trap: stops on the trap like Jar’s first slide.

**Pool:** Trapper stays Pool 5 T2. Bear Trap is Token (pool -1), not drafted.
