# Paper Fight — Scorpion

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Pool 2, Tier 1. Scorpion. 2 HP, 4 Attack. Call name Venom Sac. Ability: When you Spin me, I transform a random Attack or Block Item into a Poison Flask of equal value.

The number is the attack or block value after On Spin effects. Sai can become a Poison Flask of 2 or of 4, whichever number it is showing. Arrow Head becomes 6. Chakram is always 3, and it counts once even when both the Attack half and the Block half would qualify. Chain becomes 0 unless you had retained Block.

**Restate (what the player does):**

The wheel lands on Scorpion. Poison, Marble growth, Cleric, and the other Spin changes run. Scorpion then picks one Attack or Block item in hand at random and changes that card, in its slot, into a Poison Flask whose poison number is the attack or block number that card was showing. You did not click a target. The flask stays until you play it. You still fight Scorpion, and its Attack is still 4.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

End of the Spin that landed on Scorpion, after the other On Spin changes, before you play a card. No extra click for the transform. Scorpion is not used up. The flask copies the number once. Sai does not keep flipping on the flask. Chain does not track Block you gain later. You play the flask the way you play Poison Flask: click it, click a living neighbor, that neighbor gains that much Poison, and the flask is used up. The poison is paid on the next Spin, before that monster’s Spin ability.

Closest live card: Magicat. Magicat turns up to two Attack or Block items into Marbles with the number showing. Scorpion turns one item into a Poison Flask with that number.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- Poison Flask — the new card is that item with a different number. Click a living neighbor. Amounts add. A neighbor of Plague Doctor stays at 2. The payment is on the next Spin, before that monster’s ability, and it can kill Goblin. Anvil loses 1 from that one payment. You cannot mark the monster you are facing, so this flask cannot poison Scorpion during the fight you just started.
- Magicat — same hand pool idea, one card instead of two, and the result is a flask instead of a Marble. Only the monster you landed on transforms items.
- Sai — the flask is 2 or 4, whichever Sai is showing after this Spin. The flask does not flip when you use later items.
- Arrow Head — the flask is 6. Playing it does not also make you lose 2 HP.
- Chakram — one entry in the random pool, and the flask is 3. It is not 6 when both halves would fire, and it is not 0 when the click would do nothing.
- Chain — the flask is your Block at the moment Venom Sac reads it. Stone Wall has already put retained Block back by then. With no Block, the flask is 0.
- Echo — the fight just cleared the last Attack, so Echo is showing 0 and becomes a flask of 0.
- Marble — it has already grown by 1 this Spin. The flask is that one number. It does not keep growing.
- Helmet, Spiked Shield — the flask is the Block number they are showing (lower HP, or your highest Attack item still in hand).
- Signpost — the flask is 7. The neighbor-attack rider does not copy.
- Longbow — the flask is 4, including when the monster count is odd and you could not have clicked it.
- Double Buckler, Healing Potion, Paci-Fist, Smoke Bomb, Blood Pact — not an Attack item and not a Block item. They stay.
- Cleric — if you are missing 3 or more HP, Cleric takes your worst item during this Spin. Open question whether that take happens before the flask exists.
- Quiet Bell — a silenced Scorpion does not transform anything. You just fight 4 Attack.
- Poison already on Scorpion — if that payment drops Scorpion to 0 before Spin abilities, Venom Sac does not run.
- Grappling Hook, Damsel — a neighbor swing is not a Spin onto Scorpion.
- Recycler — the card changes in place. Recycler does not count a discard. It is not an item use, so Sai does not flip and Beads do not advance because of the transform.
- Undo — an Adventure undo of this Spin puts the old card back.
- Hornet, Queen, Wraith, Commander, Dragon pierce, Goblin, empty seat, tutorial, Developer Test — the transform is not an Attack. Scorpion’s own 4 Attack is a normal swing if you Take it. Goblin still ignores that swing and can still die to the flask’s poison later. Not in the tutorial kit.

**Generous reading:**

Cleric takes a card first. Venom Sac then uses whatever Attack or Block items you still hold, and Helmet or Spiked Shield use that later hand. A flask of 0 sits in the slot and you cannot click it, so you do not spend a card to apply 0 Poison.

**Stingy reading:**

Venom Sac changes a card first. Cleric can take the new flask if it is the worst card left. A flask of 0 can be clicked: you pick a neighbor, add 0 Poison, and the card is used up.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**
- Chakram’s on-screen number is 0 when both halves are off, or higher than 3 when a damage bonus is up. The flask has to say 3 anyway, or the card text is wrong.
- If Cleric and Venom Sac both think they go first, one of them changes a card the player already lost.
- A flask of 0 that can be clicked spends a turn for no Poison. A flask of 0 that is grey can be the only card the random pick hits, and the player watches an Attack item turn into something they cannot play.
- If the transform is treated as a discard, Recycler ticks and a full-hand curse can refuse a change that was supposed to happen in place.
- If Arrow Head’s “lose 2 HP” copies onto the flask, the flask does not match “Arrow Head is a 6.”

**What the player sees:**

The wheel stops on the scorpion. The other Spin moments play. One Attack or Block card flips into a Poison Flask, and the number on that flask is the number that card was showing (3 for Chakram, 6 for Arrow Head, 0 for Chain with no Block). Then the fight opens. Scorpion still has 4 Attack. The flask waits until they click it and click a neighbor.

**If we skip this:**

Magicat already turns Attack and Block items into Marbles on Spin. Nothing turns one of them into a Poison Flask.

**If we take the strongest reading:**

One Spin can turn Arrow Head into a flask of 6, or a grown Marble into a flask of its new number. The next Spin pays that poison before the monster’s ability. Cleric does not get to eat the flask, and a 0 flask cannot waste a click.

**Questions for Nathan:**
1. When Cleric would also take a card on this Spin, who goes first?
2. Can you click a Poison Flask of 0?

**Answers (2026-10-28):**
1. Cleric takes a card first. Venom Sac then turns one Attack or Block item you still hold into the flask. Helmet and Spiked Shield use the hand after that take.
2. A Poison Flask of 0 can be clicked. You pick a neighbor, add 0 Poison, and the card is used up.

**Collisions written down while building:**
- Plague Doctor in the live game adds 2 Poison to neighbors and freezes poison payment while he lives. The older flask sentence that neighbors stay at 2 is not what that monster does now. Venom Sac uses the live mark.
- A flask of 0 still needs a living neighbor. With no neighbor, the click is grey, same as any Poison Flask.
- Echo at the start of the fight is 0, because the last Attack was cleared when the Spin landed.
- Pool 2 already had seven monsters, including Beggar, Eel, and Lich at tier 1. Scorpion is the eighth. No other card was moved.
