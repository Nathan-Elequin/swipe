# Paper Fight — Cleric rework (Health Insurance)

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Cleric is too strong. Rework to: “After you Spin, if you are missing at least 3 HP I will Heal you for 3 and take your worst Item.” Worst-item logic and take animations stay the same. Apply all other On Spin effects first before checking Health Insurance (Cursed Scroll, Grief Jar, Harpy). Show the ability text in a flash/dissolve when it activates, along with the walk/heal/take already there.

**Restate (what the player does):**

You do not click Cleric. After a Spin finishes, and after the other On Spin cards have already done their thing, if a living unsilenced Cleric is still on the wheel and you are at 3 HP or less (missing at least 3 of 6), they Heal 3 and delete your lowest-tier item. Ties stay random. Spider Egg stays Tier 0. Empty hand still Heals, with nothing to take. You cannot click during that movie. Take / Done / item hits during a fight no longer trigger this.

**Where it happens in a round:** After Spin, once that Spin’s other On Spin effects have resolved. No click. Not used up. Heal is always 3, capped at 6. Stolen card is the current lowest-tier hand card. Quiet Bell on that Cleric turns this off. Smoke, Commander, Wick, Vampire shuffle, and Cowardly King flee are still Spins, so they can also check.

**Which existing cards this touches:**
- **Cursed Scroll / Grief Jar** — each costs 1 HP on Spin. Those hits land first. If they drop you to 3 or less and you live, Cleric can then Heal 3 and steal. If they kill you, you die and Cleric does nothing.
- **Harpy** — meet-hit on Spin lands first. If that poke drops you to 3 or less, Cleric can then pay. Harpy’s later after-item pokes are not a Spin, so they no longer trigger Cleric.
- **Hornet / Kobold / Damsel / Needleback / Dragon / Fangs** — fight chips and extra swings are not a Spin. They no longer trigger Cleric.
- **Merchant / Gelatinous Cube** — player-choice On Spin after Cleric.
- **Quiet Bell** — that Cleric does not pay.
- **Two Clerics** — the second only pays if you are still missing at least 3 after the first Heal 3.

**Generous reading:** After every Spin while a Cleric is alive on the wheel. Automatic On Spin first, then missing-3 check, then Merchant/Cube pick.

**Stingy reading:** Only when you land on the Cleric. Empty hand no Heal. Both Clerics snapshot.

**Nathan's answers:**
1. Second Cleric only pays if you are still missing at least 3 after the first Heal 3.
2. Cleric after automatic On Spin, then you pick for Merchant/Cube.
3. Yes — override locked Cleric fields with the new After-Spin text.
