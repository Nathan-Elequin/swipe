# Paper Fight — Orb

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Pool 4. Orb (crystal magic focus orb). Defeat your enemy if you are perfectly Blocking them, and Gain a Marble equal to their Attack. 0 damage and 0 Block counts. It works on the same total the Safe button uses. The Marble stays a Marble, with that monster’s sprite inside the glass. The name stays Marble.

**Answers:**

1. Tokens do not reset. Every token is deleted at the end of a dungeon.
2. When the button says Take, Orb stays grey. The click does not spend it.

**What was built:**

Click during a fight on the enemy you are facing. Legal only when incoming damage after Block is 0. Defeat uses the Boot path. The Marble’s number is the Attack on their plate, copied once. It still picks Attack or Block at random and still grows by 1 on Spin. Token items leave the hand when the dungeon clears, and again when the next dungeon is prepared.
