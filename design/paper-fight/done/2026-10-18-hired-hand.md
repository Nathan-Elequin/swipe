# Paper Fight — Hired Hand

**Pitch:** T1 B6. 4 HP 3 ATK. Bribe. Discard an item: he Attacks a random Neighbor (Pocketwatch rules), then Flees. Overlay + Decline.

**Nathan’s picks:**
1. No living Neighbors (including last on the wheel): bribe unavailable. Decline only. He does not Flee.
2. Any card in hand can be the Discard (Hex, Potion, Egg, Marble, petrified).
3. Discard is not an item use (Cube: no Harpy, Kobold, Beads, Echo).
4. Empty hand: overlay anyway, Decline only, then the snarky fight line.
