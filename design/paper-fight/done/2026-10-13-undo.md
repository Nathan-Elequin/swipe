# Paper Fight — Undo

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Players can click Undo at any point to reverse their most recent action. The only major irreversible threshold is each spin of the wheel. A main action is clicking an item and using it (including targeting and every knock-on). If they click Crossbow, pick Needleback, take reflect damage, lose health, and other enemies buff — one Undo puts the fight back to the moment before they clicked Crossbow. Snapshot whenever they could click an item. One button. Instant restore with a short reset animation.

**Restate (what the player does):** During a fight (and anywhere they can click an item), they click **Undo**. The last thing they chose to do is gone: the item is back, numbers are back, the wheel looks like it did, and they can click something else. They do not pick a target for Undo. Undo is not used up. They cannot Undo a Spin — once the wheel has landed, that landing stays. They can Undo the items they used after that landing.

**Where it happens in a round:** Whenever they could click an item (fighting, and ready if Danger Magnet / similar can be used before Spin). Not a card. Not used up. No target. The “number” is the whole board: HP, Block, the hotbar, the wheel, who they are facing, and every pending pick. One click restores the last saved board.

---

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- **Crossbow / Longbow / Throwing Knife / Hook / Pocketwatch** — click, then pick a slice. The whole pick-plus-hit is one action. Undo while they are still picking a target cancels the pick and puts the item back unclicked.
- **Chaos Stone (Mute Stone)** — click, then pick two items, then they reroll. One Undo restores both picks and the Stone.
- **Knife choose (throw vs nick)** — click, then pick an option. That whole choose is one action.
- **Needleback** — reflect, HP loss, and anything that listens to HP loss (Ogre, Vampire, Dire Wolf) all go back with the hit that caused them.
- **Harpy** — she attacks after each item. That extra attack is part of the item action, not its own Undo step.
- **Kobold Trapper / Wick** — extra swings and +1 ATK / −1 HP on item use go back with that item.
- **Basilisk** — items discarded while facing him come back on Undo of that use.
- **Gelatinous Cube** — forced feed after Spin. Player clicks an item. That click is a player action after the irreversible Spin.
- **Merchant** — forced upgrade pick after Spin. Same: a player click after Spin.
- **Gargoyle petrify / Cursed Scroll / Queen eggs / Juggernaut / Leech / Cleric / poison on Spin / Harpy poke on Spin** — these fire as part of Spin. If Spin cannot be undone, these stay.
- **Smoke Bomb** — ends the fight and calls an automatic Spin. That automatic Spin is a new wheel landing.
- **Cowardly King** — stays or flees after a fight; some readings shuffle and Spin. A new Spin is a new wall.
- **Clockwork Knight** — leaving two Tier 2 owners happens when the fight ends, not when they click an item.
- **Hornet / Queen / Dragon / Goblin / Slime / Wraith / Commander / Anvil / Twins / Damsel** — their combat math is knock-on of the hit or of Done. Undo of that action restores the board those rules changed.
- **Echo Bell / last Block / Beads** — they remember the last use. Undo must restore that memory or the next card lies.
- **Ogre / Vampire / Dire Wolf** — they listen to HP changing at any time. Restoring HP without restoring their plates would desync.
- **Empty alcove / last owner / tutorial kit / Developer Test** — Undo must still work with one slice, a tutorial wait, and the test wheel.
- **Danger Magnet** — can be used in ready, before Spin. That is an item click before the next wall.
- **Defeat overlay** — a lethal item or a lethal Done can end the run. Whether Undo still works from that screen is a code fork.

**Generous reading:** They can keep pressing Undo, one action at a time, all the way back to the board that existed the instant the last Spin finished (including Cube feed and Merchant pick). Done is an action and can be undone. If an item kills someone and the game auto-Spins, they can still Undo that item — the auto-Spin comes back too. They can Undo from the defeat screen. After Undo, random cards roll again if they retry them. Ctrl+Z works. Tutorial and Developer Test get Undo.

**Stingy reading:** One Undo only (the last completed action). Done cannot be undone. Cube feed and Merchant pick cannot be undone (they belong to Spin). If an item causes a new Spin (Smoke Bomb, auto-Spin after a slay, King shuffle), that item is locked. Death locks the run. Random results stay the same if they do the same thing again. No keyboard shortcut. Tutorial stays scripted with Undo off.

**Ways it can fail:**
- An `await` from the undone action is still running (spin tween, Harpy poke, slay) and writes on top of the restored board.
- Hotbar cards are Nodes; restoring dictionaries without rebuilding the cards leaves ghost cards or spent art.
- Echo Bell / last Block / Beads / Wick / Trapper counters restore HP but not the memory, so the next click is wrong.
- Undo during a target pick leaves `pending_mark` / `pending_chaos` live, so the next click is still “pick a neighbor.”
- Smoke Bomb or a slay auto-Spins; Undo either illegally crosses a Spin or cannot restore the item they just used.
- Tutorial `tut.wait` no longer matches the restored board; the lesson is stuck.
- Cube feed: they Undo the feed, then Done is allowed while the Cube still expects a feed.
- Developer Test / Play Latest: extra debug items and a custom wheel must come back exactly.

**What the player sees:** A single **Undo** button, grey when there is nothing to undo. Click it: a short rewind (whoosh, cards settle, numbers jump back). They are immediately allowed to click items again. They should never see a half-restored fight.

**If we skip this:** Every click stays permanent. The only “take it back” that exists today is Chaos Stone’s own cancel while picking, and Esc-style cancel on some pending picks. There is no way to un-hit Needleback.

**If we take the strongest reading:** They can walk a whole fight back to the post-Spin board, including undoing Done, undoing a lethal hit from the defeat screen, and undoing an item that already auto-Spun the next owner. One fight becomes a sandbox until they click Spin on purpose.

**How I would build it (recommendation):**
Do not write a reverse for each card. Save a full copy of the run (HP, Block, wheel, hotbar, who they face, pending picks, Echo / Block / Beads memory, silenced, combat counters, and the RNG seed) at each moment they could click an item. Undo writes that copy back, kills leftover animations, rebuilds the cards, and refreshes the board. Spin (the player’s Spin, and any new landing that counts as a Spin) clears the list of copies. A generation number aborts any `await` that belonged to the undone action. The button sits next to Done / Spin. Feel can be a short rewind in the same turn as the rules.

**Answers (2026-10-13):**
1. Keep pressing Undo back to the board right after the last Spin. Spin locks.
2. Done is not undoable (it often auto-Spins).
3. A new landing is a wall. The item that caused that Spin is locked.
4. Cube feed and Merchant pick are locked with Spin for now.
5. Yes — Undo from the defeat screen if the lethal action was an item.
6. Chaos Stone cannot be reversed (random). Completing it locks the stack.
7. Tutorial: Undo off.
8. Fight and ready only (plus defeat/victory overlay when an item killed them).
9. Button only.
10. Mid-pick Undo cancels the whole item.
11. Danger Magnet in ready is undoable.
12. Undo and Redo.
