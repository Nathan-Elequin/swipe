# Paper Fight — Rough ten (Nathan’s review notes)

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):** Apply the gold-box notes on Lantern, Mirror Shard, Net (was Caltrops), Echo Bell, Quiet Bell, Gelatinous Cube, Gargoyle, Harpy, Kobold Trapper, Clockwork Knight. Retire Caltrops. Net: click 1 / 2 / 3, subtract after every other Attack formula including Commander.

**Restate (what the player does):**
- **Lantern:** In a fight, click it. Heal 2. If HP is still below 6, the card stays. If you are at 6 after the heal, it is used up.
- **Mirror Shard:** In a fight, click it. Gain Block equal to the last Block you gained. Used up.
- **Net:** In a fight, click it, then click 1, 2, or 3. Facing Attack drops by that much after Commander and every other formula, then stays there. Used up. Caltrops leaves the dungeon.
- **Echo Bell:** In hand it becomes a copy of the last item you used (faint Echo icon). How you spend that copy is not settled.
- **Quiet Bell:** In a fight, click it. Facing owner loses their unique ability and reads as a Golem-like unit with their printed ATK/HP. Used up.
- **Cube:** Fight start. Two random items are offered. You pick one to discard with no effect (not an item use). Maybe a line of dialogue.
- **Gargoyle:** After spin numbers are done, the highest-Attack item in hand becomes Unplayable for this fight (stone overlay). Tie is random. No Attack items: nothing. Longbow and Spiked Shield count. Boot and Poison Flask do not.
- **Harpy:** At fight start, and after every item you use, she attacks you until you click Done or you Defeat/Slay her.
- **Kobold:** One extra swing per item you used this fight (0 items = 1 swing, 2 items = 3 swings). Cube feed does not count.
- **Clockwork:** If you do not Defeat or Slay them, they leave and two random Tier 2 owners take their place.

**Where it happens in a round:** Fight, except Cube / Gargoyle / Harpy’s first hit which are fight-start. Echo may rewrite a card that is sitting in hand between uses. Net and Quiet Bell are one click (Net then a 1/2/3 pick). Cube is a forced pick of two, not an item use.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- **Hornet / Queen / Kobold extras / Dragon pierce / Goblin ignore ATK / Slime ATK=HP / Commander +2 / Lich +1 HP / Wraith copy / Serpent 2-item drop / Mimic gift / empty seats / last owner / tutorial kit / Developer Test 8-full wheel** — all ten notes hit at least one of these. Quiet Bell is the one that turns those specials off. Harpy is the one that can hit you before Done. Clockwork is the one that can overflow a full wheel.
- **Caltrops** — retired. Net is the remaining Attack-shrink.
- **Throwing Knife** — Net’s second click copies this UI, but Net does not deal Attack.
- **Breastplate keep / Healing Potion** — Lantern is heal-2-then-maybe-keep.
- **Shield / Helmet / Grief Jar / Bracers** — Mirror’s “last Block gained.”
- **Smoke Bomb** — aborts the fight without Done or Slay. Petrify “until next Spin” vs “until this fight ends.” Clockwork split. Harpy cease.
- **Fangs / Boot / Sledge** — can Slay Harpy or Clockwork. If Harpy interrupts *before* the item finishes, you can die in the middle of Fangs.
- **Spiked Shield / Longbow / Medallion / Grimoire / Sai / Knife** — Gargoyle’s “has Attack” test.
- **Bandit / Wolf / Ogre / Skeleton / Clockwork formulas** — Quiet Bell’s “printed base stats.”
- **Old Cube (rightmost auto-eat)** — replaced by pick-two.

**Generous reading:** Start order is Cube pick, then petrify, then you get a turn before Harpy hits. Harpy each time is one swing of her current Attack, after the item fully resolves (Fangs can kill her first). Echo sits in hand as the last item and you click it to fire that copy; if nothing used yet, you cannot click it. Quiet Bell lasts this fight only. Lantern at 6 HP cannot be clicked. Mirror with no Block gained this fight cannot be clicked. Cube with 0 items does nothing; with 1 item that item is the only feed. Clockwork’s two Tier 2s sit in empty seats after the fight; if the wheel is full they wait for empty seats. Petrify clears on the next Spin (Smoke included). Net floors at 0.

**Stingy reading:** Harpy hits at start before you can click anything, and “resolve combat” means full Done (all extra swings, they leave). Echo is a one-shot of last item even from the previous fight. Quiet Bell lasts the rest of the dungeon. Lantern at full HP is spent for 0 heal. Mirror at 0 last-Block is spent for 0. Cube with 1 item still forces the discard. Clockwork dumps two Tier 2s even onto a full wheel (or fails silently). Petrify lasts until Done/Slay of *that* Gargoyle, so Smoke does not clear it if you land on them again.

**Ways it can fail:**
- Harpy + Cube + Gargoyle all fire on fight start in an order that kills you, petrifies the card you needed, or asks you to pick from an empty hand.
- Harpy “resolve combat” ends the fight on the first item, so you never get Done.
- Echo copies Knife’s second click after Echo is already gone from the hand.
- Echo copies Echo, or copies a Keep card and duplicates it forever.
- Quiet Bell on Bandit with a blank ATK column leaves Attack 0 for the rest of a dungeon.
- Clockwork on an 8-owner wheel has nowhere to seat two more, or reseats you into a new fight immediately.
- Net 1/2/3 pick still waiting after the card is discarded.
- Petrify + Cube: the only Attack item is stone, Cube offers it, feeding it is the only way to drop it.

**What the player sees:** Wheel Attack/HP, hotbar, Done. Cube needs two item portraits and a feed click. Gargoyle needs a stone overlay and a Shift line for Petrify. Echo needs a small bell on the copied card. Harpy needs to be obvious that she just hit you without Done. Clockwork’s two new owners need to appear on the wheel in a way that is not a second combat in the same click.

**If we skip this:** The ten stay on the old rules (Cube eats rightmost, Caltrops sets 0, Harpy is Attack = Block, etc.).

**If we take the strongest reading:** Harpy full-resolves on spin (fight over before items). Echo copies any last item including Keep. Clockwork always adds two owners even at 8. Quiet Bell is dungeon-long. That pack can end a run from one spin plus one item.
