# Paper Fight — Trapper

Paused 2026-10-28 so Flying Saucer could use CURRENT.md. Questions were not answered yet.

**Pitch (Nathan’s words):**

Rework Kobold Trapper. Rename to Trapper. 3 HP, 3 ATK. Call name Hunter's Mark. Ability: When I enter the Dungeon I Mark 2 other Monsters. Each time a Marked monster is Defeated, I gain +2 HP and ATK. Marked hover: "upon Defeat, Trapper gains +2 HP and ATK". Picks 2 other monsters at random when entering, or 1 or 0 if there are not enough. Never itself. Overlay does not cover HP or ATK. Two Trappers each gain from even a single marked target. Bag of Holding counts. Catapult does not.

**Restate (what the player does):**

The player does not click a target. When Trapper is in the dungeon at the start of the fight, or when Trapper is added later, the game marks up to two other monsters. The player sees an overlay on those monsters and can hover it. When one of those monsters is Defeated, Trapper's HP and ATK on the plate each go up by 2. Trapper is not used up. The card id stays `kobold_trapper`.

**Where it happens in a round:** phase / used up or kept / must they click a target? / is the number copied once or does it keep updating?

Enter, same moment as Beggar's mark: when the fight can be played, and again if this monster is added later. No click. The +2 is stored on that Trapper and stays, including after Flee, the same way Vampire keeps gained Attack and Leech keeps gained HP. A hurt Trapper keeps the missing HP: 1/3 becomes 3/5, and ATK goes from 3 to 5. It is not an aura that peels off at the end of the fight.

**Which existing cards this touches:**

- Beggar — same "when I enter, mark something at random" timing. Beggar's mark moves and clears. This mark stays until that monster is Defeated.
- Bag of Holding — already runs the Defeat rewards, then removes the monster. This pays.
- Catapult — Eject. Puts them on the bottom of the deck. This does not pay.
- Phoenix — dying is a Defeat (egg is laid in that same step). The death pays. The egg is a new monster and is not Marked.
- Vampire / Leech / Fairy dust — the pattern for a number that stays on the monster and survives a later stat refresh.
- Quiet Bell — silence recomputes most monsters back to their printed numbers, and it clears Beggar's mark until the silence ends.
- Wraith — copies the Defeated monster's most recent HP. A Marked monster's HP does not change. A Trapper who already grew, then dies, would hand over the grown HP.
- Flee — the monster stays on the wheel and keeps permanent stat changes. A Trapper who Fled still has the grown numbers. Whether he still receives new +2s after Flee is open.
- A second Trapper — he said both gain from even a single marked target. That sentence still fits two different payments.
- Goblin, Hornet, Queen, Dragon, empty seats — they do not change the payment. Defeat is the trigger, not the hit. Fewer than two other monsters marks fewer. He already said that.

**Generous reading:**

Every Trapper still on the wheel, including ones you have not reached and ones that Fled, gains +2 HP and +2 ATK whenever any Marked monster is Defeated. Quiet Bell clears the marks and stops new growth, and it also puts this Trapper back to 3 HP and 3 ATK. A monster marked twice still pays only +2 to each Trapper.

**Stingy reading:**

Only the Trapper you are facing grows, and only from a monster that Trapper personally marked. If both marked the same one, both grow. Quiet Bell does nothing to marks already placed or to numbers already gained, because the mark sits on the other monster. Two marks on the same monster pay +4.

**Ways it can fail (crash, stuck game, card text that does not match the hit):**

- Inspect says Hunter's Mark while the fight still uses the old extra swings. That is true until this is coded.
- A Marked monster is removed without Defeat (Flee, Catapult) and the overlay stays forever, or the overlay vanishes and a later real Defeat never pays.
- Two Trappers mark overlapping sets and one death pays the wrong number.
- Quiet Bell wipes the grown HP in the middle of the fight, or leaves the overlay up while the ability text is blank.
- The overlay covers the HP and ATK numbers.
- The word Mark in the ability has no hover sentence. He defined Marked only.

**What the player sees:**

Trapper's plate is 3 HP and 3 ATK. On enter, up to two other monsters get an animated overlay that leaves the numbers readable. Hovering that overlay says: upon Defeat, Trapper gains +2 HP and ATK. After a Marked Defeat, that Trapper's numbers are 2 higher. Bagging the marked monster does this. Catapulting it does not.

**If we skip this:**

Kobold Trapper stays a 2 ATK / 6 HP monster who swings once plus once per item used.

**If we take the strongest reading:**

One Marked Defeat grows every Trapper on the wheel by +2/+2, including ones you have not fought yet, and a second mark on the same monster pays again. Two Trappers and two marks on one Goblin would be +4 HP and +4 ATK on each Trapper from that one death. That does not end the fight by itself. It makes later Trappers much larger.
