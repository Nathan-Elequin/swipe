# 01_GEMINI_title_still

**1. Open:** https://gemini.google.com/app (model: Flash or Pro). Pick a wide/landscape image if asked.

**2. Prompt** (copy all of it):

```
Style: crisp retro pixel art like a 16-bit SNES/GBA game for the dark-fantasy dungeon game "Spinheritance". Every art pixel is a hard-edged square of one flat colour on one consistent grid. 1-pixel near-black outline (#100e0c) on characters and objects. 3-4 tone colour ramps, light from the upper-left. No anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no soft glow, no dithering noise, no painterly or 3D look. Gold is #FFD84A. Stay inside the colours of the palette image. Never draw any text, letters, numbers or watermarks.

Make the title screen illustration for Spinheritance: a wide landscape pixel-art painting, about 420x260 art pixels (each art pixel a big crisp square). Image 1 is the current title art: keep its layout idea and night palette, but redraw it as version 3 with these changes.

The Loot River: night. Mount Grumblegut fills the back, dark blue-grey under a starry sky. In its side a huge cave mouth with stone teeth glows torch-gold from inside. Out of the mouth a river of treasure pours down toward the lower left, like a waterfall turning into a stream.

The treasure is NOT a messy pile. It is 8 big, clearly separate, readable artifacts riding the river, each 2-3 times larger than a coin, with a thin light stream of gold coins flowing behind them. Match image 4, left to right: 1 a gold pocketwatch on a chain, 2 a black iron lantern with a lit flame, 3 a red leather grimoire with a gold clasp, 4 a round red healing potion with a green cork, 5 a great double-bladed axe with red-steel blades, 6 a silver-and-gold chakram ring blade, 7 a gold chalice brimming red, 8 a big silver lucky coin with a green emblem leading the coin stream.

The hero, Sprout, a small goblin, stands on a rock ledge in the upper-left third, leaning forward and reaching one green hand toward the nearest artifact, mouth open in greedy delight. Sprout must be the SAME character as images 2 and 3: green skin, long pointed ears, big pointed nose, teal hooded cloak, red scarf/belt, burlap loot sack on the back, small and stocky. Draw Sprout bigger and more detailed (about 48-64 art pixels tall) but keep that exact design and those colours.

Monsters line both banks of the river, all facing RIGHT toward the mountain mouth and the loot, each with a big expressive face and attitude and a distinct silhouette, based on image 5: a sneering green goblin, a snarling grey dire wolf, a grinning skeleton with a dagger, a huge green ogre with a club on his shoulder, a buzzing hornet swarm, a purple spider queen with a gold crown, a wooden mimic chest with its tongue out, a grey stone gargoyle, a smug purple wizard-hat cat, a blue poison frog puffing its throat, a crowned lich in grey robes. Vary their sizes; the ogre and spider queen are the biggest. From the right edge a giant red dragon claw reaches in, talons spread, grabbing at the treasure (claw and part of the arm only).

Grandpa is shown only by his objects, never as a living figure: his cream safari pith helmet with a red band tumbling in the river, his gold-framed portrait (image 6) leaning on a rock by the bank, and his burlap sack (image 7) spilling coins.

Lower-left: a small translucent ghostly cabin on a hill, pale blue and see-through, with one warm window.

Layout rules: keep the centre of the picture (middle third, from about 25% to 75% down) calm and low-detail: dark river and coins, no faces. Keep the top 30% mostly sky and mountain with clear space in the middle for a logo. Put Sprout and the strongest faces in the upper-left, the outer left and right thirds, and the lower corners.

No text, no letters, no UI, no border, no logo. Opaque background. Use image 8 as the colour palette.
```
