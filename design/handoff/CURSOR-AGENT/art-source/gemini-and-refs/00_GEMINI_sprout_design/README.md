# 00_GEMINI_sprout_design

**1. Open:** https://gemini.google.com/app (model: Flash or Pro)

**2. Prompt** (copy all of it):

```
Character design sheet for the hero of a cozy-funny pixel-art dungeon game. Pixel art, crisp 1px dark outline (#100e0c), 3-4 tone shading lit from the upper left, no anti-aliasing, no text, flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) background. The hero is Sprout, a young goblin heir: small and chibi (head about 40% of total height), big round friendly eyes with a highlight, a small rounded nose, short perky ears, a hopeful earnest little smile, rosy cheeks. Keep the colour identity from image 1 and image 2: green skin, teal hood and short cloak (no long cape), red scarf, a brown striped tunic, and a small sack tied at the hip. Brave but a little nervous, someone you root for, not a sneaky thief. Show 4 views in one row, evenly spaced and the same size, feet on the same baseline: front, 3/4 front, side facing right, back. Plus one extra pose: happily holding up a shiny gold coin. Use the colours from image 3.
```

**3. Attach:** Select all images in this folder (3) and drop them into the chat.

**4. Save the result as:** `..\returns\sprout_design_raw.jpg`

**5. If it's off** (paste in the same chat, one at a time):
- `Cuter: bigger eyes, smaller nose, rounder head, shorter body.`
- `Same design, but make every view the exact same size and proportions.`
- `Remove the long cape; keep the cloak at waist length.`
