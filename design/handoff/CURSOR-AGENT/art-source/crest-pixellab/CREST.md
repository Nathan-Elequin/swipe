# Option B crest variants (2026-10-20)

The ability crest is centred on the name tag and set into a notch in the tag's bottom border.
- Tag and crest are one unit at 3x Nearest, the same scale as the HP/ATK badges.
- Each icon is baked into its crest at native pixel size, so there is no mixed pixel size inside a crest.
- HP heart is on the left and ATK star on the right, in B, B1, B2 and the A–D layouts.

## B1: medallion
- A 22px coin sits in a semicircular bite with a gold lip, which reads as set into the tag.
- The icon sits on a dark field, so it reads well at wheel size.

## B2: shield
- A 20x23 shield drops into a square socket and looks more heraldic.
- The icons on the crimson field are murky at 1x, especially the bolt and the horn.

## Fit
- The crest adds 52px (B1) or 57px (B2) below the tag.
- Top and bottom slices: the block stays centred at 0.60R.
- Side slices: the block moves out to 0.645R and 16px away from the horizontal divider.
- Result: no hits with badges, neighbouring sprites or the hub. The closest gaps are:
  - about 20px between a side-pair crest and the sprite below it
  - about 25px between a top crest and the hub ring
