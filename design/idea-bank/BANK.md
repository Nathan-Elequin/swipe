# Idea bank — 2026-10-07 designer experiment

Offline store for this repo. Grounded in the live roster (ATK, HP, BLK, wheel seats, neighbors, opposite, items in hand, last fight, kills, bleed, Keep, Defeat, extra swings, pierce, thorns) and in Welcome to the Dungeon, Munchkin, One Deck Dungeon, and similar lightweight fantasy combat.

## What the current roster is doing

Every live toy copies, inverts, or taxes a number the player can already see. Boot cares about HP vs HP. Sledgehammer cares about ATK 5+. Bandit is items in hand. Serpent pays you for two spends. Dragon ignores Block. Goblin ignores Attack. Hornet and Queen spend Block twice. Needleback pokes when you cut. Mill loot invites a matching-tier owner. Monsters speak in first person. There is no Munchkin “plus to combat,” no levels, no curses-as-deck-hate.

That is the constraint I treated as design law: one click, one verb, one live number, and a collision with extra swings / copy-ATK / last-in-calc / pierce / unhittable / empty seats.

## Similar games (mechanics, not names to steal)

### Welcome to the Dungeon

Monsters ranked Goblin → Dragon by strength. Equipment is mostly a one-shot tax (you take the item, so the dungeon is scarier). Distinct toys: Vorpal Blade (outright kill), Polymorph (swap the monster), Gelatinous Cube (eats an item), Mimic / Shapeshifter, Ring of Power, Invisibility. The interesting layer is not “another sword.” It is “this object changes which number still matters.”

### Munchkin

Slot limits, one-shots, curses, “this monster is extra against wizards,” silly named loot. Most of that fights this game: we do not have classes, levels, or plus-to-combat. Keep only the toys that become a visible number here (discard a card, skip a swing, tax the hand).

### One Deck Dungeon

Hero dice vs dungeon dice. Drain / Fade / Frost / Split / Undying. Closest cousins here: freeze ATK to 0, copy the last hit, a monster that refuses a keyword.

### Other lightweight fantasy combat

Pocket-dungeon / push-your-luck crawls: skip a room, eat loot, scale with how empty the map is, punish turtling, punish spending.

## Cut to 10 — wave 2 (implemented 2026-10-08)

Previous ten moved to **refined**. New ten from the leftover likely list, avoiding verbs the first ten already occupy (Lantern is Heal-Keep, Quiet strips uniques, Clockwork is owners-left Attack, Kobold is extra swings, Harpy is interrupt).

### Items

1. **Chain** — Attack = Block you hold, then Block 0. Missing convert-Block-to-Attack. Punishes Hornet: you dump soak, extras land clean.
2. **Counting Beads** — Attack = empty alcoves (8 − owners left). Lantern no longer uses this number. Inverse Clockwork: good late, dead on a full wheel.
3. **Last Match** — Defeat if this is your only Item. Third Defeat key after Boot (HP) and Sledge (ATK 5+). Gargoyle still says no.
4. **Measuring Tape** — Attack = |your HP − their HP|. Ogre gap 0. Bloody Gauntlets is HP 7 compare; this is the difference.
5. **Mute Stone** — Extra Done swings become 1. Quiet Bell already strips the whole unique. This is the small version: Hornet/Queen/Kobold only.

### Monsters

6. **Basilisk** — Keep fails this fight. Taxes Knife nick, Lantern, Breastplate.
7. **Hollow** — If you click Done at 0 Block and live, they are Slain. Inverse turtle. Smoke does not count (no Attack).
8. **Beggar** — Attack 6 if hand empty, else 1. Inverse Bandit. Updates when you spend.
9. **Wick** — +1 Attack per item use this fight. Kobold adds swings; Wick adds the number.
10. **Anvil** — Max 1 Attack from a single Item. Knife 3 becomes 1. Defeat still works.

## Cut to 10 (wave 1, now refined)

### Items

1. **Lantern** — Attack = owners left on the wheel. Leech already uses that count for HP. This is the Attack version. You want it late, when the wheel is small, or you spend it early as a modest chip. Goblin ignores it.

2. **Mirror Shard** — Block = facing Attack. Full soak of one swing unless they pierce or swing twice. Dragon still hits you. Hornet spends the Block on swing one. That is the Block-vs-extra-swings lesson in a single card.

3. **Caltrops** — Facing Attack becomes 0 this fight. Inverse of Serpent (you spend one Item, they lose the swing). Dragon pierce of 0 is 0. Harpy: this locks Attack at 0 even if you later gain Block, because Caltrops writes after formulas. Kobold still swings twice for 0.

4. **Echo Bell** — Attack = last Item Attack this fight. Grimoire copies last monster Attack. This copies your last spend. Forces a sequence: chip, then echo. Cannot be the first spend.

5. **Quiet Bell** — Skip their swing; they still leave. Smoke rerolls and they stay. Fangs makes you take the hit. This is the third exit: they are gone, you do not take the hit, you do not reroll. Deadly because it trivializes Dragon and Hornet for one fight. Gargoyle can be removed this way (Defeat is blocked; skip-and-leave is not Defeat).

### Monsters

6. **Gelatinous Cube** — On spin, the rightmost Item is discarded. Welcome to the Dungeon's cube, written in this game's hand. Punishes a fat hotbar. Does not count as “you used an Item,” so Serpent does not drop and Kobold does not double from the eat.

7. **Gargoyle** — Cannot be Defeated. Inverse Goblin. Boot, Sledgehammer, Fangs fail. Attack to 0 still works. Quiet Bell still removes them. Needleback-style poke does not apply.

8. **Harpy** — Attack equals your Block this fight, and it updates when you gain Block. Turtling is the trap. Dragon pierce is irrelevant if you hold 0 Block. Mirror Shard into Harpy is a bad loop (you gain their Attack as Block, they grow). Caltrops zeros them.

9. **Kobold Trapper** — Extra swing if you used at least one Item. Inverse Serpent. The knife is a tax. Empty-hand combat is the safe line. Echo Bell is an Item, so it doubles them.

10. **Clockwork Knight** — Attack = owners still on the wheel (locked when you spin them). Leech uses that count for HP. This uses it for Attack. Fight them last (Attack 1) or eat them early with Defeat / Quiet Bell. Commander still adds +2 after.

### Why these ten together

Five player verbs that were missing: count the wheel, copy their Attack into Block, write Attack to 0, copy your own last spend, skip the swing.

Five owner verbs that punish the five habits the live roster teaches: fat hand, Defeat kit, Block stack, Item spam, saving the scary seat for last.
